Your money is at stake, so let's be clear about how payment works, how to manage deposits, and how to protect yourself.

## How payment usually works

- **Directly to the hospital** — the safest route. Pay the hospital's **international department** by card, bank transfer, or mobile payment (Alipay/WeChat Pay with a linked foreign card).
- **Deposit** — for some procedures, a hospital may ask for a **deposit** to reserve your slot. Confirm the amount and **refund policy** in writing.
- **Itemized bill** — you should receive a **written, itemized bill** (in English) for every payment.

## Protecting yourself — the basics

1. **Pay the hospital directly**, not a third party, wherever possible.
2. **Get a written, itemized quote before you pay** — no quote, no payment.
3. **Confirm the deposit amount and refund policy in writing.**
4. **Keep every receipt and bill** (paper + digital) — essential for insurance and disputes.
5. **Use traceable payment methods** (card, bank transfer, mobile pay) — avoid large cash payments.
6. **Verify the hospital is a real, accredited institution** (see [Quality & Safety](/cmedical/quality-safety/)).
7. **Be wary of "too good to be true" prices** — unusually cheap offers can hide excluded costs or an unverified provider.

## Red flags (possible scams)

- A provider **won't give a written, itemized quote**.
- Pressure to **pay a large sum immediately** or to a personal/unverifiable account.
- A "hospital" you **can't independently verify** (no official website, no address, no accreditation).
- Prices **far below** realistic market rates.
- Requests to pay via **untraceable methods** (cryptocurrency, wire to a private account).
- A "middleman" who **disappears** after taking your money.

## If you suspect a problem

- **Stop further payments** until it's resolved.
- **Document everything** — messages, receipts, names.
- **Contact us** — we can help verify the hospital and coordinate.
- **Contact your bank** if a card payment is disputed.
- **Contact your embassy/consulate** for urgent matters.
- **Notify your insurer** if relevant.
