Cancer costs are highly individual, depending on the cancer type, stage, and treatment modality. These are **broad planning figures** — always get a written, itemized plan from the oncology team.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Treatment | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Standard chemotherapy (per cycle) | 500 – 3,000 | Number of cycles multiplies cost |
| Chemoradiation | 1,000 – 8,000 | Depends on modality and sessions |
| Targeted therapy (per month) | 1,500 – 10,000 | Drug varies |
| Immunotherapy (per infusion) | 1,000 – 5,000 | Drug varies |
| Proton therapy (full course) | 20,000 – 40,000 | At dedicated centers |
| CAR-T cell therapy | 30,000 – 40,000+ | Selected blood cancers |

## What affects the cost
- **Cancer type and stage**
- **Treatment modality** (standard vs. advanced)
- **Number of cycles/sessions**
- **Hospital stay** and supportive care

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **exact treatment plan** and number of cycles
- **Drug costs** (targeted/immunotherapy drugs vary widely)
- **Supportive care** (anti-nausea, blood products, etc.)
- **Follow-up and monitoring** after treatment

> ⚠️ Cancer costs are among the most variable. Standard chemo is often significantly cheaper than in the West, while advanced therapies (CAR-T, proton) are higher-cost and specialized. Get a written estimate for your case.
