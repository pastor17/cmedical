Indicative cost ranges for common heart procedures in China. These are **planning figures** — always get a written, itemized quote from the hospital for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Coronary angiography | 500 – 2,000 | Diagnostic catheterization |
| Single stent (PCI) | 2,000 – 8,000 | Stent brand and number vary |
| Multiple stents | 4,000 – 15,000 | More vessels, higher cost |
| Bypass surgery (CABG) | 5,000 – 25,000 | Complexity and stay length |
| Valve surgery | 8,000 – 40,000 | Mechanical vs. biological valve |
| Electrophysiology (ablation) | 3,000 – 15,000 | Procedure type |

## What affects the cost
- **Stent/valve brand** (domestic vs. imported differ)
- **Hospital and city** (top-tier urban centers cost more)
- **Number of vessels/procedures**
- **Length of hospital stay** and any complications

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **device brand** (stent/valve) and its cost
- Whether **follow-up visits** are included
- **Medication** after the procedure (often separate)

> ⚠️ Prices vary by case and change over time. Figures are indicative only.

**Compare** these ranges with your home country — cardiac procedures in China are typically **substantially less** than in the US/UK for comparable care.
