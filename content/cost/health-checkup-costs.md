Indicative cost ranges for health screening packages in China. These are **planning figures** — get a written quote for the specific package you want.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Package | Indicative US$ range | What's typically included |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Basic checkup | 200 – 500 | General exam, blood work, chest X-ray |
| Standard checkup | 500 – 1,000 | Adds ultrasound, ECG, more blood tests |
| Comprehensive executive | 1,000 – 3,000 | Adds CT, cancer markers, cardiac tests |
| Premium / full-body | 3,000 – 5,000+ | Adds MRI, endoscopy, specialized tests |

## What affects the cost
- **Number and type of tests** included
- **Hospital/center level**
- **Add-ons** (MRI, endoscopy, specialized imaging)

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **exact list of tests** in the package
- Whether the **report is in English**
- **Fasting requirements** before the visit
- **Follow-up** for any abnormal results

> ⚠️ Packages and prices vary by center and change. Figures are indicative only.
