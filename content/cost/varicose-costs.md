Indicative cost ranges for vein (vascular) treatment in China. These are **planning figures** — confirm a written quote for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Sclerotherapy | 500 – 1,500 | For smaller veins; per session |
| Laser/radiofrequency ablation | 2,000 – 6,000 | Per leg/vein, varies |
| Surgical vein procedure | 3,000 – 10,000+ | For extensive cases |

## What affects the cost
- **Extent** of the veins being treated
- **Technique** (laser, RF, sclerotherapy)
- **Number of legs/veins**
- **Hospital/center and city**

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **assessment** (duplex ultrasound) and **procedure**
- **Per leg vs. total**
- **Compression stockings** (often prescribed)

> ⚠️ Prices vary by case and change. Figures are indicative only.
