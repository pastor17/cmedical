Indicative cost ranges for spine surgery in China. These are **planning figures** — always get a written, itemized quote for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Discectomy / microdiscectomy | 5,000 – 15,000 | For a herniated disc |
| Spinal fusion | 10,000 – 30,000+ | More complex; implant cost varies |
| Laminectomy | 5,000 – 15,000 | For spinal stenosis |
| Minimally invasive spine (MISS) | 5,000 – 20,000 | Technique varies |

## What affects the cost
- **Procedure complexity** and number of levels
- **Implant/rod** cost (varies by brand)
- **Hospital and city**
- **Length of stay**

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **procedure** and **number of levels**
- The **implant** brand and cost
- **Follow-up** and rehabilitation
- **Complication** costs

> ⚠️ Costs vary by case and change. Figures are indicative only.
