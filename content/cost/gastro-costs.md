Indicative cost ranges for gastroenterology and endoscopy in China. These are **planning figures** — confirm a written quote for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Upper endoscopy (gastroscopy) | 200 – 800 | Diagnostic |
| Colonoscopy | 300 – 1,000 | Diagnostic; more with biopsy |
| ERCP | 1,000 – 5,000 | For bile-duct/pancreatic issues |
| Treatment (e.g., polyp removal) | 500 – 2,000 | Added to the procedure |

## What affects the cost
- **Procedure type** and whether **treatment/biopsy** is done
- **Sedation** (often included)
- **Hospital/center and city**

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **procedure** and any **treatment** included
- **Biopsy** costs if applicable
- The **report** (in English)

> ⚠️ Prices vary by center and change. Figures are indicative only.
