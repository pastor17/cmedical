Indicative cost ranges for bariatric (weight-loss) surgery in China. These are **planning figures** — always get a written, itemized quote for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Gastric sleeve | 8,000 – 20,000 | The most common bariatric procedure |
| Gastric bypass | 10,000 – 25,000+ | More complex, higher cost |
| Gastric balloon | 2,000 – 6,000 | Non-surgical, reversible |

## What affects the cost
- **Procedure type** (sleeve vs. bypass)
- **Hospital and city** (top-tier urban centers cost more)
- **Length of stay** and complexity
- **Pre-surgical evaluation** costs

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **procedure** and whether the **evaluation** is included
- **Follow-up** visits (often ongoing)
- **Medication and vitamins** after surgery
- **Complication** costs if the case is more complex

> ⚠️ Costs vary by case and change. Figures are indicative only.
