Transparent, indicative cost ranges for common dental work in China. These are **planning figures** — always get a written, itemized quote for your case.

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| Dental checkup + cleaning | 50 – 300 | Exam, X-rays, professional cleaning |
| Single crown | 200 – 800 | Depends on material (metal, zirconia) |
| Single implant (all-in) | 500 – 1,500 | Implant + abutment + crown; brand varies |
| Full mouth / multiple implants | 2,000 – 10,000+ | Depends on number of teeth and grafting |
| Orthodontics (braces) | 500 – 3,000 | Varies by type and duration |
| Root canal | 150 – 600 | Per tooth, varies by tooth and complexity |
| Bone graft / sinus lift | 300 – 1,500 | Add-on when bone is insufficient |

## What to confirm in a quote

- The **implant/crown brand** and material
- Whether **follow-up visits** are included
- **Warranty** on the work
- **Extractions, grafting, or other add-ons** you may need

> ⚠️ Prices vary by city, hospital/clinic level, and individual case. Figures are indicative and subject to change.

**Compare** these ranges with your home country’s costs — dental in China is typically **50–80% less** than in the US/UK/Australia for comparable quality.
