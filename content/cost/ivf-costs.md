Indicative cost ranges for fertility treatment in China. These are **planning figures** — confirm a written, itemized quote for your specific case.

> ⚠️ **Regulatory note:** IVF eligibility rules in China are specific and can change. **Confirm current rules and your eligibility directly with the center before planning a trip.**

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Item | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|------|----------------------|-------|
| One IVF cycle (base) | 3,000 – 6,000 | Includes monitoring, retrieval, lab, transfer |
| Medications (per cycle) | 500 – 2,500 | Varies by individual response |
| ICSI (advanced fertilization) | 500 – 1,500 | Add-on |
| Embryo freezing | 300 – 1,000 | Per year of storage varies |
| Additional cycle | 3,000 – 8,000 | Not every cycle results in pregnancy |

## What affects the cost
- **Center and city**
- **Medications used** (individual response varies)
- **Number of cycles**
- **Add-ons** (ICSI, freezing, genetic testing)

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **full cycle cost** including medications
- Whether **ICSI or add-ons** are needed
- The **realistic success rate** for your age/profile
- **Multiple-cycle** budgeting (not every cycle succeeds)

> ⚠️ Prices vary by center and change. Figures are indicative only.
