Joint replacement (knee and hip) is a high-volume, well-established procedure in China, performed by experienced surgeons. Here are indicative cost ranges to help you plan.

> ⚠️ These are **planning figures**, not a quote. Confirm a written, itemized estimate from the hospital for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| Single knee replacement | 3,000 – 12,000 | Implant brand, hospital level, stay length |
| Single hip replacement | 3,000 – 12,000 | Varies by implant and hospital |
| Bilateral (two) knees | 5,000 – 20,000 | Two implants, longer stay |
| Revision surgery | Higher | Complex, case-dependent |

## What affects the cost

- **Implant brand** (domestic vs. imported brands differ)
- **Hospital and city** (top-tier urban hospitals cost more)
- **Length of stay** and complexity
- **Physiotherapy** included or separate

## What to expect

- **Inpatient stay:** typically several days to a week, plus rehab.
- **Physiotherapy** is a key part of recovery — ask how it’s arranged and billed.
- **Follow-up** visits after discharge.

> 💡 China’s joint-replacement cost is typically **well below** US/UK prices for comparable implants. Use the [Cost Estimator](/cmedical/estimator/) for a ballpark, or [start a consultation](/cmedical/contact/).
