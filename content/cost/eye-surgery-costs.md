Indicative cost ranges for common eye surgeries in China. These are **planning figures** — confirm a written quote from the hospital for your case.

## Indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | Indicative US$ range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| LASIK / SMILE (refractive) | 300 – 2,500 | Per pair; technique varies |
| Cataract surgery | 500 – 3,000 | Per eye; lens type varies |
| Glaucoma surgery | 1,000 – 5,000 | Procedure type varies |
| Retina / vitreoretinal surgery | 2,000 – 10,000 | Case-dependent |

## What affects the cost
- **Lens/technique type** (for cataract and refractive)
- **Hospital/center and city**
- **Per eye or per pair**
- **Complexity** of the case

## What to confirm in a quote
- The **exact technique/lens** included
- Whether **follow-up visits** are included
- **Post-op eye drops** and their cost

> ⚠️ Prices vary by center and change. Figures are indicative only.
