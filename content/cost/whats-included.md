A **written, itemized quote** is the single most important document before you travel. But quotes aren't created equal — what's **included** and what's **excluded** varies. Here's how to read one and what to ask.

## What a good quote usually includes

- **Consultation fee** — the specialist's first review
- **Surgical/procedure fee** — the main treatment
- **Anesthesia fee** — for the procedure
- **Hospital stay** — the room(s) and ward for the nights you're in
- **Tests & diagnostics** — blood work, imaging (X-ray, CT, MRI), ECG, etc.
- **Implant/device** (if applicable) — the implant, stent, valve, or IOL (with brand)
- **Medication** — in-hospital medication (sometimes post-discharge is separate)
- **Physiotherapy** — in-hospital sessions (often a set number)

## What it often excludes (ask about these)

- **Travel & airfare** — your flights to/from China
- **Accommodation** — hotel or apartment during your stay
- **Interpreter** — language support (we arrange this)
- **Transport** — airport and city transfers
- **Follow-up visits** — post-discharge appointments (in China or at home)
- **Post-discharge medication** — sometimes billed separately
- **Imaging/records copies** — extra films or digital copies
- **Additional procedures** — anything found during surgery not in the original plan
- **Emergency care** — unrelated to the planned treatment
- **Tips/gratuities** — not required, but customary in some contexts

## The questions to ask about a quote

When you get a quote, confirm **each** of these in writing:

1. Is this an **all-in** price, or are there separate charges?
2. What **specifically is included** in the price?
3. What is **not included** and how much would it cost?
4. What is the **implant/device brand** (and its cost if separate)?
5. How many **follow-up visits** are included?
6. Is there a **deposit**, and is it refundable?
7. What is the **payment schedule** (when do I pay each part)?
8. What happens if the **procedure is more complex** than expected — how much more?
9. Are **post-discharge medications** included?
10. Can I get the **itemized bill** and records in English for insurance?

> 💡 **Get it in writing.** A verbal quote is not a quote. Ask for an **itemized, written** document (in English) before you commit.

> ⚠️ Prices and what's included vary by hospital and case. This is general guidance. **Always confirm in writing with your specific hospital.**

## Why this matters

A clear quote protects you from **surprises**. If a hospital won't give a detailed written quote, that's a red flag. A good international department will happily provide an itemized breakdown and explain it in English.
