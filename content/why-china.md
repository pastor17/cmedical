Many international patients choose China for medical care. Here's a balanced, honest look at why — and what to consider.

## Quality of care

- **World-class hospitals.** China's top **Grade III-A** hospitals (the highest tier) use modern equipment, follow international standards, and treat enormous patient volumes.
- **High-volume expertise.** In many procedures, Chinese specialists perform more cases than their counterparts abroad — which often means faster, more refined techniques.
- **Strong specialties.** China is especially recognized for cataract surgery, joint replacement, cardiac intervention, organ transplant, and a growing range of cancer therapies.

## Cost

For comparable care, treatment in China is often **substantially less** than in the US, UK, or Australia. This is one of the main reasons patients travel.

> Indicative examples (case-dependent — always get a written quote):

| Procedure | China (US$, indicative) | Typical US/UK (US$, indicative) |
|-----------|------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Cataract surgery | 500 – 3,000 | 3,000 – 5,000+ |
| Single stent | 2,000 – 8,000 | 30,000 – 100,000+ |
| Knee replacement | 8,000 – 20,000 | 30,000 – 50,000+ |
| Dental implants | 500 – 1,500/tooth | 2,000 – 5,000/tooth |

> These are broad planning figures. **Get a written, itemized quote** from the hospital for your case.

## What to consider

- **Language** — international departments provide English support and interpreters (we arrange these).
- **Regulations** — some treatments (e.g., IVF, transplant) have specific rules; confirm eligibility.
- **Travel** — plan for the journey, visa, and stay (see our [Travel guide](/cmedical/travel/)).
- **Aftercare** — arrange medication continuity and follow-up (see [Aftercare](/cmedical/travel/aftercare/)).
