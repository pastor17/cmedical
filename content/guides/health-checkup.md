Comprehensive health checkups (health screening) are a major and very affordable part of care in China. They combine modern diagnostics into a single, efficient visit. Here's what to expect.

## What a comprehensive checkup includes
- **General exam** — blood pressure, weight, and vital signs
- **Blood work** — complete blood count, liver/kidney function, glucose, lipids, and more
- **Imaging** — chest X-ray, ultrasound (abdomen), and often CT
- **Cardiac** — ECG, and sometimes echocardiography
- **Cancer screening** — tumor markers and targeted imaging
- **Optional add-ons** — MRI, endoscopy, specialized tests

## How it works
- **One day (or a short stay)** — most checkups are done in a single visit.
- **Results & report** — you receive a written report, often with a doctor's summary.
- **English report** — many hospitals can provide an English version on request.

## What to plan for
- **Fasting** before the visit (typically 8–12 hours) for blood work.
- A **review of your report** — ideally with a doctor who can explain findings.
- **Follow-up** for any abnormal results.

## Costs (varies by package)
> **Indicative:** A basic package may be **US$ 200 – 500**; a comprehensive executive package **US$ 500 – 3,000+**, depending on the tests included.
