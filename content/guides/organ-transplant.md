China has established organ transplant programs at leading specialized centers, with formal organ allocation systems. This is an overview to help you understand the process.

> ⚠️ **Organ transplant is complex and heavily regulated.** Eligibility, donor availability, and process are governed by national rules that can change. **Confirm current regulations and eligibility directly with the transplant center before planning anything.**

## Main programs
- **Liver transplant** — one of the most common solid-organ transplants.
- **Kidney transplant** — including living-related and deceased-donor.
- **Heart and other organ transplants** — at specialized centers.

## The general process
1. **Evaluation** — a comprehensive medical assessment to determine suitability.
2. **Listing & matching** — entering the organ allocation system; waiting for a suitable donor.
3. **Surgery** — the transplant itself.
4. **Post-transplant care** — immunosuppression, monitoring, and follow-up.

## What to plan for
- A thorough **pre-transplant evaluation** (may require travel).
- The **wait and matching** process, which can be uncertain.
- **Long-term follow-up** and medication management.
- **English records** for continuity of care.

## Costs (highly case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Total cost including surgery, hospital stay, and initial follow-up can range from roughly **US$ 20,000 – 90,000+**, depending on the organ, complications, and length of care.

These are broad planning figures. Get a written estimate from the center.
