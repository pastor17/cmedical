China’s major cancer centers are among the busiest and most advanced in the world, combining high-volume surgical teams with rapidly growing cell and gene therapies. This is a neutral overview to help you understand the options and plan.

> ⚠️ **Cancer care is deeply individual.** This is general information, not medical advice. A treatment decision must be made with your treating oncology team, based on your specific diagnosis and staging.

## Common treatment modalities

- **Surgery** — for solid tumors, often combined with other therapies.
- **Chemotherapy** — standard for many cancers.
- **Targeted therapy** — drugs aimed at specific molecular markers in the tumor.
- **Immunotherapy (PD-1/PD-L1)** — increasingly available and widely used.
- **CAR-T cell therapy** — a rapidly advancing option for certain blood cancers; China is one of the more active regions for CAR-T development.
- **Proton / heavy-ion therapy** — advanced radiation for selected cases, available at dedicated centers.
- **Radiation & supportive care.**

## Choosing the right center

- Look for a **comprehensive cancer center** (Grade III-A) with a multidisciplinary team (MDT).
- Match the center to **your cancer type** and whether you need a specific therapy (e.g., CAR-T).
- Consider the **international department** for English service and coordination.

## Costs (very case-dependent)

> **Indicative:** A course of treatment can range from roughly **US$ 2,000 for standard chemo to US$ 40,000+ for advanced cell therapy**, depending on the modality, number of cycles, and hospital stay.

- Standard chemo/chemoradiation is often significantly cheaper than in the West.
- **CAR-T and proton therapy** are higher-cost, specialized treatments — confirm availability and pricing for your case.

> ⚠️ Cancer costs are highly individual. Get a written, itemized plan and quote from the hospital.

## Tips for international patients

- Bring your **full pathology and staging** for the first consultation.
- Ask about **second opinions** and MDT review for complex cases.
- Confirm the **treatment timeline** (inpatient stays, cycles) to plan travel.
