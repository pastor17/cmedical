Fertility treatment is a major draw for international patients: China’s IVF centers are advanced, high-volume, and considerably more affordable than in the West. This is an overview to help you understand the process and plan.

> ⚠️ **Regulatory note:** IVF rules in China (including rules on who may be treated and the use of donor gametes) are specific and can change. **Confirm current regulations and eligibility directly with the center before planning a trip.**

## How IVF typically works

1. **Consultation & evaluation** — medical history, exams, hormone tests.
2. **Ovulation stimulation** — medications to develop multiple eggs.
3. **Egg retrieval** — a minor procedure under sedation.
4. **Fertilization & embryo culture** — in the lab.
5. **Embryo transfer** — embryos placed in the uterus.
6. **Pregnancy test & follow-up.**

A single cycle usually takes **2–3 weeks on site**, plus travel for consultations and a pregnancy test.

## What it usually costs

> **Indicative range (per IVF cycle): roughly US$ 3,000 – 8,000**, depending on the center, medications, and the number of embryos.

Costs vary by:
- **Center and city**
- **Medications used** (can vary significantly by individual response)
- **Number of cycles** (not every cycle results in pregnancy)
- **Add-ons** (ICSI, embryo freezing, etc.)

> ⚠️ Planning figures only. Get a written, itemized estimate for your case.

## Tips for international patients

- Choose a center with an **international patient program** and English service.
- Ask for a **full cycle cost** including medications.
- Understand the **realistic success rates** for your age and diagnosis.
- Plan for **possible multiple cycles** in your budget and timeline.
