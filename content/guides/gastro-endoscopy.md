Gastroenterology is a major specialty in China, with widespread endoscopy services. Here's an overview.

## Common services
- **Upper endoscopy (gastroscopy)** — for stomach and upper GI issues
- **Colonoscopy** — for colon screening and diagnosis
- **ERCP** — for bile-duct and pancreatic issues
- **Treatment of** — acid reflux (GERD), ulcers, IBS, and other GI conditions

## The typical process
1. **Assessment** — history, and preparation for endoscopy.
2. **Procedure** — endoscopy/colonoscopy, often with sedation.
3. **Results** — with a written report (biopsy if taken).
4. **Follow-up** for any findings.

## What to plan for
- **Fasting** before the procedure (instructions provided).
- **Bowel preparation** for colonoscopy.
- **A companion** if sedation is used.
- **English report** of findings.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Endoscopy/colonoscopy commonly ranges from **US$ 200 – 1,500** depending on the procedure and whether treatment/biopsy is done.
