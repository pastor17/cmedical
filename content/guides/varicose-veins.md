Vein treatment (vascular surgery) is a well-developed specialty in China. Here's an overview of common conditions and treatments.

## Common conditions
- **Varicose veins** — enlarged, twisted veins
- **Spider veins**
- **Deep vein issues** — including clot (DVT)

## Common treatments
- **Minimally invasive** — laser or radiofrequency ablation
- **Sclerotherapy** — for smaller veins
- **Surgical** — for more extensive cases
- **Compression therapy** — stockings and lifestyle

## The typical process
1. **Assessment** — examination and **duplex ultrasound** of the veins.
2. **Treatment plan** — based on the severity and location.
3. **Procedure** — often minimally invasive, as a day-case.
4. **Follow-up** — to check results.

## What to plan for
- **Compression stockings** (may be provided/prescribed).
- **Activity** — walking after the procedure is usually encouraged.
- **Follow-up** to monitor.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Vein treatment ranges from **US$ 500 – 2,000** for simpler treatments to **US$ 3,000 – 10,000+** for more extensive procedures, depending on the case.
