Joint replacement (knee and hip) is a high-volume, well-established procedure in China, performed by experienced surgeons. Here's what to expect.

## The procedure
A damaged joint surface is replaced with an implant (metal/plastic components). Most are done with spinal or general anesthesia and often use minimally invasive techniques.

## Typical timeline
- **Pre-op workup** — bloods, scans, and optimization of your health.
- **Surgery** — the replacement itself.
- **In-hospital stay** — typically several days to a week, with physiotherapy starting early.
- **Recovery** — continues at home and in outpatient follow-up.

## What affects cost and length
- **Implant brand** (domestic vs. imported differ in price)
- **Hospital and city** (top-tier urban centers cost more)
- **Length of stay** and complexity
- **Physiotherapy** — a key part of recovery

## What to plan for
- A **physiotherapy program** — recovery is won in rehab.
- **Follow-up visits** after discharge.
- **English records** for continuity with your home doctor.
