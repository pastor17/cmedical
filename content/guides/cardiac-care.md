Cardiac care is one of China's strongest specialties. Major heart centers perform very high volumes of complex procedures with modern equipment. This is a neutral overview to help you understand the options and plan.

> ⚠️ **Heart care is time-sensitive and individual.** This is general information, not medical advice. A treatment decision must be made with your treating cardiology team. In a cardiac emergency, call local emergency services immediately.

## Common procedures

- **Coronary intervention (PCI / stents)** — opening blocked arteries; a high-volume procedure in China.
- **Coronary bypass surgery (CABG)** — for complex multi-vessel disease.
- **Valve repair/replacement** — for heart valve disease.
- **Electrophysiology** — ablation and device procedures for rhythm problems.
- **Imaging & diagnostics** — angiography, CT, echocardiography.

## Choosing the right center
Look for a **heart center** (a dedicated cardiac department within a Grade III-A hospital) with a multidisciplinary team and high procedure volume. Match the center to your specific condition (e.g., complex coronary vs. valve).

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** A stent procedure may range from roughly **US$ 2,000 – 8,000**, while bypass surgery can be **US$ 5,000 – 25,000+**, depending on complexity and stay.

These are planning figures. Get a written, itemized plan and quote from the hospital.

## Tips for international patients
- Bring your **prior cardiac tests and imaging** for the first consultation.
- Confirm the **procedure plan and timeline** (some require pre-op workup).
- Ask about **post-procedure follow-up** and medication continuity at home.
