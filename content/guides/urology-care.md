Urology is a strong specialty in China, with many high-volume centers. Here's an overview of common conditions and treatments.

## Common conditions & procedures
- **Kidney & bladder stones** — often treated with minimally invasive or laser techniques
- **Prostate** — for enlarged prostate (BPH) and other conditions
- **Urologic cancer** — bladder, kidney, prostate
- **Reconstructive** — for various urologic issues

## The typical process
1. **Diagnosis** — imaging, urine tests, and sometimes cystoscopy.
2. **Treatment** — from medication to minimally invasive or surgical.
3. **Follow-up** — to monitor results.

## What to plan for
- Bring your **prior imaging, urine tests, and history**.
- A **clear discussion** of options (conservative vs. surgical).
- **Follow-up** and any lifestyle changes.
- **English records** for continuity.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Urology procedures range from **US$ 500 – 2,000** for common treatments to **US$ 5,000 – 25,000+** for complex surgery, depending on the case.
