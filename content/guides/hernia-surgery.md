Hernia repair is a common, well-established surgical procedure in China. Here's an overview.

## Common types
- **Inguinal hernia** — the most common
- **Umbilical hernia** — at the belly button
- **Incisional hernia** — at a previous surgery site
- **Femoral hernia**

## The procedure
A hernia occurs when tissue pushes through a weak spot in the muscle wall. Repair usually involves **pushing the tissue back** and reinforcing the area, often with a **mesh**. It can be done **open or laparoscopically**.

## What to expect
- **Pre-op assessment** — including any relevant tests.
- **Surgery** — often as a day-case or short stay.
- **Recovery** — gradual return to activity; avoid heavy lifting for a period.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Hernia repair commonly ranges from roughly **US$ 2,000 – 8,000**, depending on the type, approach (open vs. laparoscopic), mesh, and stay.
