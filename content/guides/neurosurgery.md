Leading neurosurgical teams in China handle a high volume of complex cases. This is a neutral overview of the main areas of neuro care and how to plan.

> ⚠️ **Neurological conditions are serious and individual.** This is general information, not medical advice. Stroke and other acute neurological emergencies require immediate care — call local emergency services.

## Main areas
- **Brain tumors** — diagnosis and surgical removal, often with advanced imaging and navigation.
- **Spine surgery** — disc, deformity, and spinal tumor surgery.
- **Stroke** — acute and chronic management.
- **Epilepsy** — diagnosis and treatment, including surgical options.
- **Vascular** — aneurysm and vascular malformation treatment.

## The typical process
1. **Diagnosis** — imaging (CT/MRI), and sometimes functional testing.
2. **Multidisciplinary review** — for complex cases.
3. **Treatment** — surgery, medication, or a combination.
4. **Rehabilitation** — a key part of neuro recovery.

## What to plan for
- Bring your **prior imaging and history** for the first consultation.
- A **clear understanding of the diagnosis and options** — ask direct questions.
- **Rehabilitation planning** as part of recovery.
- **English records** for continuity with your home neurologist.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Neurosurgery ranges widely — from roughly **US$ 3,000 – 30,000+** depending on the procedure, complexity, and length of stay.
