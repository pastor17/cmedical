Dental implants are among the most popular reasons international patients come to China — the same treatment at a fraction of the cost found in the US, UK, or Australia. Here’s what to expect.

## What a dental implant is

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth’s root with a titanium post, onto which a crown is attached. A single implant typically involves:
1. Examination & planning (X-ray / CT scan)
2. Implant placement (surgery, usually local anesthesia)
3. Healing / osseointegration (weeks to months)
4. Abutment & crown placement

## Typical timeline

A single implant often spans **2–4 months** from first visit to final crown, because the implant must integrate with the jawbone. Some patients do the initial work in China and complete later stages at home; others do it all in one trip with a staged plan.

## What it usually costs

> **Indicative range (single implant, all-inclusive estimate): roughly US$ 500 – 1,500** depending on the implant brand, the city, and any additional work (bone grafting, extractions, crown type).

Costs vary widely by:
- **Implant system** (different brands have different prices)
- **Number of teeth** and whether bone grafting or sinus lift is needed
- **Crown material** (e.g., zirconia)
- **Hospital/clinic level**

> ⚠️ These are planning figures, not a quote. Get a written, itemized estimate from the hospital for your specific case.

## Tips for international patients

- Choose a clinic/hospital with an **international department** and English service.
- Ask for the **full package price** and what’s included (implant, abutment, crown, follow-ups).
- Confirm the **implant brand** and warranty.
- Ask about **follow-up visits** after you return home.
