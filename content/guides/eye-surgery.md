China's eye centers, especially in Chengdu and Shanghai, are world-class and high-volume. This is an overview of the main types of eye surgery and what to expect.

## Main types
- **Refractive surgery (LASIK / SMILE)** — correcting nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism.
- **Cataract surgery** — replacing a clouded lens; very common and efficient in China.
- **Retina / vitreoretinal surgery** — for retinal detachment, macular disease, and other retinal conditions.
- **Glaucoma surgery** — for elevated eye pressure.

## What to expect
- A pre-op **eye examination** (refraction, imaging, health review).
- The **procedure** itself is often quick (minutes to an hour) with local or surface anesthesia.
- **Follow-up** visits to check healing.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Refractive surgery is often **US$ 300 – 2,500**; cataract surgery **US$ 500 – 3,000** per eye; retinal surgery higher and case-dependent.

These are planning figures. Get a written estimate from the hospital.

## Tips for international patients
- Bring your **prior eye tests and prescription** if you have them.
- Ask about **post-op eye drops and follow-up** plans.
- For refractive surgery, confirm **eligibility** (corneal thickness, prescription stability).
