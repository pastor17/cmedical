ENT (otolaryngology) is a major specialty in China, and some of China's specialized ENT centers are among the busiest in the world. Here's an overview.

## Common procedures
- **Tonsillectomy / adenoidectomy** — for recurrent tonsils/adenoids
- **Sinus surgery (FESS)** — for chronic sinusitis
- **Hearing / cochlear implants** — for hearing loss
- **Nasal surgery** — deviated septum, polyps
- **Throat & voice** — for voice and swallowing issues

## What to expect
- A **specialized examination** (endoscopy, imaging, hearing tests).
- The **procedure** — many are routine and efficient.
- **Follow-up** to check healing.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** ENT procedures vary widely — from **US$ 500 – 2,000** for common procedures to **US$ 5,000 – 30,000+** for complex surgery (e.g., cochlear implants).

These are planning figures. Get a written quote.

## Tips for international patients
- Bring your **prior ENT records and hearing tests** if available.
- Ask about **post-op care and follow-up** plans.
- For hearing procedures, confirm **eligibility and device** details.
