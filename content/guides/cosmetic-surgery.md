Cosmetic and plastic surgery is a popular choice for international patients in China, with experienced surgeons and accredited centers. This is an overview to help you plan safely.

## Common procedures
- **Facial** — rhinoplasty, blepharoplasty (double-eyelid), facelift
- **Body** — liposuction, breast augmentation, body contouring
- **Skin** — laser resurfacing, non-surgical treatments
- **Reconstructive** — post-trauma or post-surgical reconstruction

## Choosing a center (safety first)
- Choose **accredited hospitals or clinics** with a strong plastic surgery department.
- Confirm the **surgeon's credentials and case numbers** for your specific procedure.
- Ask about **before/after examples** and realistic expectations.
- Understand the **complication and revision** policy.

## What to plan for
- A **consultation** to discuss goals and risks.
- **Pre-op workup** and any anesthesia assessment.
- **Recovery time** — some procedures need a week or more before flying.
- **Follow-up** visits after the procedure.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** A single procedure may range from roughly **US$ 500 – 8,000+** depending on the procedure, surgeon, and center.

These are planning figures. Get a written, itemized quote.

> ⚠️ **Safety matters.** Cosmetic surgery carries real risks. Choose accredited providers, ask direct questions, and allow adequate recovery time before traveling.
