Bariatric (weight-loss) surgery is a major, well-established specialty in China, offered at leading hospitals with dedicated metabolic/obesity programs. Here's an overview.

> ⚠️ **Bariatric surgery is a major operation** with specific eligibility criteria and lifelong follow-up. It must be planned carefully with a specialist.

## Common procedures
- **Gastric sleeve (sleeve gastrectomy)** — removing part of the stomach
- **Gastric bypass (Roux-en-Y)** — rerouting the stomach and intestine
- **Gastric balloon** — a non-surgical, reversible option (less common)

## Who it's for
- Typically for adults with a **high BMI** (eligibility criteria vary) and/or obesity-related conditions.
- A **comprehensive evaluation** (medical, psychological, nutritional) is standard before surgery.

## The typical process
1. **Evaluation** — health assessment, bloods, and a multidisciplinary review.
2. **Preparation** — diet and lifestyle optimization before surgery.
3. **Surgery** — usually laparoscopic (keyhole).
4. **In-hospital stay** — a few days, with early diet progression.
5. **Lifelong follow-up** — diet, vitamins, and regular check-ups.

## What to plan for
- A **thorough pre-surgical evaluation**.
- **Lifelong dietary and nutritional** changes (vitamins, portion sizes).
- **Follow-up** — in China and/or remote, after you return.
- **Realistic expectations** — it's a tool for weight management, not a cure.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Bariatric surgery commonly ranges from roughly **US$ 8,000 – 25,000+**, depending on the procedure, hospital, and stay.
