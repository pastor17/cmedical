Dermatology is a strong specialty in China, with both medical and cosmetic skin care. Here's an overview.

## Common services
- **Medical dermatology** — acne, eczema, psoriasis, and other skin conditions
- **Procedures** — for moles, warts, and skin lesions (with biopsy where needed)
- **Cosmetic dermatology** — laser resurfacing, pigment, and other treatments
- **Hair** — for hair loss

## The typical process
1. **Consultation & examination** — of the skin and your history.
2. **Tests** — e.g., biopsy where needed for diagnosis.
3. **Treatment** — medication, procedures, or a plan.
4. **Follow-up** to monitor.

## What to plan for
- Bring your **history of the condition** and any prior treatments.
- A **clear discussion** of diagnosis and options.
- **Aftercare** instructions.
- For cosmetic procedures, **realistic expectations** and a written plan.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Dermatology ranges from **US$ 50 – 500** for consultations/medical treatment to **US$ 1,000 – 10,000+** for procedures/cosmetic treatments, depending on the case.
