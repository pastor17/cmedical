Spine surgery is a high-volume, well-developed specialty in China. Here's an overview of the main procedures and what to expect.

> ⚠️ **Spine surgery is serious and individual.** This is general information. Make decisions with your treating team, and in an acute neurological emergency (e.g., loss of bladder control, progressive weakness) seek immediate care.

## Common procedures
- **Discectomy / microdiscectomy** — for a herniated disc causing nerve pain
- **Spinal fusion** — for instability or severe degeneration
- **Laminectomy** — for spinal stenosis (narrowing)
- **Spinal deformity correction** — for scoliosis, kyphosis
- **Minimally invasive spine surgery (MISS)** — smaller-incision techniques

## The typical process
1. **Diagnosis** — MRI/CT, and sometimes functional or endocrine tests.
2. **Conservative care** — often tried first (physio, medication) if appropriate.
3. **Surgery** — if conservative care fails or there's a clear indication.
4. **Rehabilitation** — a key part of recovery.

## What to plan for
- Bring your **prior imaging and history**.
- A **clear discussion** of the diagnosis, options, and expected outcome.
- **Rehabilitation planning** as part of recovery.
- **English records** for continuity with your home doctor.

## Costs (case-dependent)
> **Indicative:** Spine surgery ranges widely — roughly **US$ 5,000 – 30,000+**, depending on the procedure, complexity, and stay.
