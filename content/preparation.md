Good preparation is what makes your trip go smoothly and your treatment effective. Here's how to get medically ready before you fly.

## 1. Gather your medical records

The more you bring, the better the first consultation. Collect:

- **Diagnosis and history** — written summary of your condition
- **Imaging** — X-rays, CT, MRI, ultrasound (as **files/DICOM** on a USB if available, plus reports)
- **Lab results** — blood tests, pathology reports
- **Prior treatments** — what you've tried, and how you responded
- **Current medications** — a full list (name, dose, frequency)
- **Allergies** — drugs, foods, contrast, latex
- **Past surgeries & procedures**
- **Family medical history** (if relevant)

> 💡 **Get copies in English** if your original records are in another language. We can help translate key documents into Chinese for your doctors (and provide English copies for your home doctor).

## 2. Fitness to fly

- **Ask your doctor if you're fit to fly** — especially if you have a recent cardiac, respiratory, or surgical condition.
- **Manage your condition** before travel (e.g., optimize blood pressure, glucose).
- **Long flights** — stay hydrated, move around, and follow your doctor's advice on blood clots (especially after surgery).
- **Carry a medical summary** in your hand luggage (see below).

## 3. Vaccinations

- Check if you need any **travel vaccinations** for China (most are not required, but confirm for your situation).
- If you're immunosuppressed or have a chronic condition, **consult your doctor** before travel about any vaccines and timing.
- There are generally **no entry vaccination requirements** for most visitors to China, but verify current rules.

## 4. What to carry in hand luggage

Keep these **with you on the plane** (not in checked baggage):

- **A written medical summary** (in English) — your condition, key diagnoses, allergies
- **Current medications** in **original packaging** (plus a translated list)
- **A copy of your passport, visa, and insurance** details
- **Emergency contact** for a family member or our support
- **Your medical ID card** (we can prepare one in Chinese)

## 5. Before you leave

- **Confirm your appointment and quote** in writing.
- **Sort out your visa** (see [Visa guide](/cmedical/travel/visa/)).
- **Arrange your trip logistics** — flights, accommodation, transport, interpreter (we can do this).
- **Notify your insurer** if applicable.
- **Pack** — see our [Packing Checklist](/cmedical/packing-checklist/).
