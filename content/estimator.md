## How to read an estimate

- **The range is broad on purpose.** A simple case at the low end; a complex case with a long hospital stay at the high end.
- **Always get a written quote** from the hospital for *your* specific diagnosis, procedure, and expected length of stay.
- **Ask what’s included and excluded** — e.g., is the implant device cost included in a dental quote? Are post-op checkups included?

> ⚠️ **Important:** These figures are indicative and for planning only. They are **not** price guarantees and **not** medical advice. Prices change, vary by hospital and city, and depend on your individual case. Confirm all costs in writing before treatment.
