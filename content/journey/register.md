“挂号 (guà hào)” — getting a doctor’s appointment — is the step that trips up most international patients, because China’s booking systems are built around WeChat mini-programs and Chinese phone numbers. Here’s how to navigate it.

## The two booking paths

### Path A — Book through the International Department (easiest for foreigners)
Top hospitals run an **International Medical Department** that handles bookings for foreign patients, often in English.
1. Email or call the international desk (contact details are on each hospital page).
2. State your condition, preferred specialist/department, and preferred dates.
3. They confirm the slot and usually handle the in-hospital logistics too.

### Path B — Book directly via the hospital app / mini-program
For simpler cases or lower cost, you can book through the hospital’s own app or WeChat mini-program.
1. **Get a Chinese phone number** (SIM or eSIM) — required for registration.
2. Install the app and register with your **passport** (foreign ID is accepted, though facial recognition can occasionally fail for foreign faces).
3. Pick the department and time slot, then pay the registration fee.

## Understanding the registration levels

| Level | What it is | When to use |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| 普通号 (general) | Standard outpatient consult | Routine first visits, follow-ups |
| 专家号 (expert) | Senior specialist consult | Complex cases, diagnosis, surgery planning |
| International dept. | English-service consult | Foreign patients who want language support |

## What to bring on the day
- Passport (or ID) + appointment confirmation
- Any prior medical records and imaging (CD/DICOM)
- List of current medications
- A companion or interpreter if you prefer extra help

> 💡 **If the app won’t let you register** (a common issue with passport + facial recognition), fall back to **Path A** — just email or call the international department. It takes longer but it works.
