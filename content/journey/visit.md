You’ve booked and prepared — now the day itself. Here’s what to expect, from arriving at the hospital to getting your first results.

## The outpatient day

1. **Arrive early** — 30–60 minutes before your appointment time.
2. **Check in** at the reception or registration desk with your ID and appointment confirmation.
3. **See the doctor** — the consultation, questions, and any immediate orders for tests.
4. **Tests & imaging** — blood work, scans, etc. may be done same-day or scheduled for later.
5. **Pay** at the payment window (WeChat/Alipay) and collect prescriptions or schedule follow-ups.

## If you’re admitted (inpatient)

1. **Admission** — paperwork, a deposit (住院押金), and assignment of a ward and doctor.
2. **Treatment** — surgery or procedures, with a nursing team.
3. **Daily rounds** — the medical team reviews your progress.
4. **Discharge settlement** — final cost reconciliation; overpayment refunded.
5. **Discharge** — you receive a discharge summary and follow-up instructions.

## Communication & logistics

- **Interpreter / companion:** if you’re not confident in English communication, a bilingual companion or hospital interpreter makes a real difference. Many international departments offer this; some patients bring their own.
- **Family visits:** know the hospital’s visiting hours if someone is staying with you.
- **Phone:** keep your Chinese number active for appointment reminders and test-result alerts.

## Practical tips

- Bring a small bag of essentials (medications, charger, comfortable clothes) if you expect a long day.
- Write down what the doctor says — or record the consultation if the hospital allows it.
- Confirm how and when you’ll receive your **test results** (app, portal, or in person).

> 💡 **Keep a paper trail.** Photograph receipts, notes, and your schedule. You’ll want these for insurance claims and follow-up.
