Getting care in China as a foreigner feels overwhelming until you see it as a sequence of clear steps. This is that sequence: **choose → book → consult → pay → visit → recover**, with a **prepare** stage for everything before you fly.

Each page below is a self-contained guide: what to do, what to bring, what it costs, and what to watch out for. Start at the step you’re at right now.

> 💡 **New here?** Start with [Choose a Hospital](/cmedical/journey/choose/) — the right hospital makes every later step easier.
