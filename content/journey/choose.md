Choosing the right hospital is the single most important decision you’ll make. Get this right and everything after — booking, communication, results — gets dramatically easier.

## What to look for

1. **Accreditation matters.** Prioritize **Grade III-A (三级甲等, “three-A”)** hospitals — the top tier in China’s hospital rating system. International accreditation such as **JCI** is an extra signal of patient-safety standards that many foreign patients prefer.
2. **Match the specialty, not just the brand.** A hospital can be world-famous overall but weaker in your specific condition. Check the hospital’s strengths for *your* diagnosis.
3. **English-language service.** Ask whether the hospital has an **International Medical Department (国际医疗部 / International Center)** — this is the channel built for foreign patients, with English-speaking staff and interpreters.
4. **Specialist’s case numbers.** For surgery and complex care, the doctor’s *volume* matters. A surgeon who has performed 5,000 of a procedure is very different from one with 50.

## A practical shortlist process

1. Identify the **right department** for your condition (see our specialty pages).
2. Shortlist 3–5 hospitals in your target city that are Grade III-A with an international department.
3. For each, check: accreditation, strengths in your specialty, and whether they accept international patients.
4. Compare: location, cost ballpark, and communication options.
5. **Contact the international department** to confirm they can handle your case and request a quote.

## Red flags to avoid

- ⛔ Hospitals that only accept payment through unverified third parties with no paper trail.
- ⛔ No willingness to give a written cost estimate or treatment plan.
- ⛔ Pressure to decide quickly or pay upfront with no records.

> 💡 **Tip:** Most top hospitals’ international departments will respond in English by email. Start there — it’s simpler than navigating a Chinese hospital app.
