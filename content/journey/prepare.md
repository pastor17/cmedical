Everything before you board the plane. A little preparation here prevents most of the stress that comes later.

## 1. Visa

- Medical visitors typically use a **medical (S2) visa** or a visitor visa — requirements vary by nationality.
- Start the visa process early; you may need an invitation or confirmation from the hospital.
- Keep copies of all medical documents handy — some visa processes ask for them.

## 2. Medical records & translation

- Gather your full medical history, imaging, and lab results.
- Decide whether to translate key documents into Chinese (recommended for smooth care).
- Make digital copies (photos/PDF) in addition to physical copies.

## 3. Travel & accommodation

- Book flights with buffer time — you may need rest before a procedure.
- Choose accommodation **near the hospital** to make appointments and follow-ups easier.
- Arrange **airport pickup** if available (some hospital services or we can help).

## 4. Money & payments

- Set up **WeChat Pay / Alipay** with a foreign card (see [Payment & Cost](/cmedical/journey/pay/)).
- Bring some **RMB cash** for small payments.
- Confirm your **insurance** covers the trip and understand the reimbursement process.

## 5. What to pack

- Passport & copies, visa documents
- All medical records & imaging (physical + digital)
- Current medications + a written list
- Comfortable clothes (for hospital stays), basic toiletries
- Phone, charger, portable charger, adapter
- Any personal comfort items for a long stay

> 💡 **Build in rest.** Travel + time-zone shift before a procedure is a lot. If possible, arrive a day or two early to settle in.
