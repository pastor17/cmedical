Cost is one of the top reasons patients choose China — but the payment process can be confusing if you’ve never paid at a Chinese hospital. Here’s how it actually works.

## How to pay

### WeChat Pay / Alipay with a foreign card
Since recent policy changes, **foreign-issued Visa and Mastercard can be linked to WeChat Pay and Alipay** — this is now the smoothest way to pay at most hospitals.
1. Install WeChat or Alipay.
2. Add your foreign card as a payment method (follow the in-app steps; there may be a small fee and per-transaction limits).
3. Pay at the hospital by scanning the QR code at the payment window.

### Other options
- **Cash (RMB)** — useful for small payments and places without card terminals.
- **UnionPay card** — a China-issued card is accepted everywhere without issues.
- **Bank card / credit card** — some international departments accept international cards directly.

## Hospital payment flow

| Type | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| 门诊缴费 (outpatient) | Pay for consultations, tests, and procedures as you go |
| 住院押金 (inpatient deposit) | A deposit paid when admitted; settled at discharge |
| 出院结算 (discharge settlement) | Final reconciliation — any overpayment is refunded |

## Insurance: two models

1. **Direct billing** — the hospital bills your international insurer directly. **Few** Chinese hospitals offer this for foreign patients; confirm before you rely on it.
2. **Reimbursement (most common)** — you pay upfront, then claim from your insurer afterward. Keep every document:
   - 发票 (fapiao / official invoice)
   - Itemized charges (费用明细)
   - Discharge summary & medical records
   - Diagnosis certificate

> ⚠️ **Ask in advance:** confirm your insurer’s rules and whether they accept Chinese hospital invoices. Not every policy covers foreign hospitalizations the same way.

## What to expect on cost

- Get a **written quote** before treatment, and ask what is **included and excluded**.
- Costs vary by hospital, city, complexity, and length of stay — a simple case and a complex case can differ many-fold.
- Use our [Cost Estimator](/cmedical/estimator/) for an indicative range, and browse [Cost Guides](/cmedical/cost/) for common procedures.

> 💡 **The #1 cost mistake:** assuming the quoted price includes everything. Always ask for a line-item breakdown and what happens if the stay or treatment extends.
