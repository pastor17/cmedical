A little preparation goes a long way. Here's what to pack and arrange before you fly.

## Documents (keep in hand luggage)

- [ ] **Passport** (valid 6+ months beyond your return)
- [ ] **Visa** (for China)
- [ ] **Travel/health insurance** details
- [ ] **Medical records** — diagnosis, history, imaging (files on USB), lab results
- [ ] **Medication list** (name, dose, frequency) — translated
- [ ] **Written medical summary** (in English) of your condition & allergies
- [ ] **Medical ID card** (we can prepare in Chinese)
- [ ] **Appointment confirmation & itemized quote**
- [ ] **Emergency contacts** — family, our support line, your embassy

## Medications & health

- [ ] **Enough of your current medications** for the whole trip + follow-up
- [ ] **Any new prescriptions** discussed pre-travel
- [ ] **Basic first-aid** — pain relief, antihistamine, bandages, thermometer
- [ ] **Any medical devices** (e.g., inhaler, glucose meter) — carry in hand luggage
- [ ] **Copies of prescriptions** in your luggage

## Comfort & practical

- [ ] **Comfortable clothing** (easy to change for procedures)
- [ ] **Loose-fitting clothes** for after a procedure
- [ ] **Supportive shoes**
- [ ] **A light jacket** (air-conditioning/seasonal)
- [ ] **Personal items** — toiletries, charger, adapter (China uses **Type A/C/I, 220V**)
- [ ] **A small bag** for day-to-day carry
- [ ] **Reusable water bottle** (fill from filtered taps)

## Money & tech

- [ ] **Some cash** (RMB) for small purchases
- [ ] **International card(s)** (Visa/Mastercard)
- [ ] **Alipay or WeChat Pay** set up with a linked card (for daily spending)
- [ ] **Phone + SIM/eSIM** (data) + offline maps of your city
- [ ] **Chargers & universal adapter**
- [ ] **Portable battery**

## Before you leave

- [ ] **Flight tickets** confirmed
- [ ] **Accommodation** booked near the hospital
- [ ] **Transport** arranged (airport + city)
- [ ] **Interpreter** arranged (we can)
- [ ] **Visa** approved
- [ ] **Insurer notified** (if applicable)
- [ ] **A trusted contact** knows your itinerary
