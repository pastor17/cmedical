A **remote (tele) consultation** lets you get a second opinion, a treatment plan, and a cost estimate **before you travel** — saving time and money. Here's how it works and what to prepare.

## Why start remotely
- **Get a second opinion** from a senior specialist before committing to travel.
- **Know the plan and cost** upfront, so you can budget and plan your trip.
- **Save time** — avoid a trip only to find the case isn't suitable or the plan is more complex.

## What you'll need to prepare
- **Medical records** — diagnosis, history, and relevant notes
- **Imaging** — X-rays, CT, MRI (as files, DICOM if available)
- **Lab results** — blood tests, pathology reports
- **Current medications** — a list of everything you're taking
- **Your questions** — what you specifically want answered

## How it works
1. **Send your records** — [via our contact form](/cmedical/contact/) or email.
2. **We match you with a suitable specialist** for your condition.
3. **The review** — the specialist (with an interpreter if needed) reviews your case.
4. **You receive** — a written opinion, a treatment plan, and a cost estimate (in English).

## What you get
- A **clear written opinion** (in English)
- A **treatment plan** with options
- An **indicative cost quote**
- Guidance on **whether travel is worthwhile** and how to prepare

## After the remote consult
If you decide to come to China, we **coordinate the whole trip** — visa, interpreter, transport, accommodation — building on the plan you already have.
