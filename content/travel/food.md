Eating well during treatment matters. Here’s practical guidance for your visit.

## Eating around treatment
- **Before and after procedures:** follow your doctor’s dietary instructions (e.g., fasting before surgery, soft foods after).
- **During recovery:** balanced, easy-to-digest food helps healing. Ask for guidance if you have dietary restrictions.

## Communicating dietary needs
- Use a **translated diet card** (we can provide) listing your restrictions/allergies in Chinese.
- Hospital **international wards** and nearby hotels can often cater to dietary needs — confirm when booking.
- **Restaurants:** English menus are common in hotels and busy areas. Point-and-show is an easy backup.

## What to expect
- Chinese food is diverse — from noodle shops and rice dishes to Western options in hotels.
- **Spiciness varies** by region (e.g., Sichuan/Chengdu is spicier; Cantonese/Guangzhou is milder).
- **Bottled water** and safe eating at reputable places is the norm.
