Your care doesn’t end at the airport. Here’s how to plan a smooth transition home.

## Medication continuity
- Ask your doctors for a **written medication plan** (what, dose, how long).
- Request **sufficient medication** for the journey and the follow-up period.
- For ongoing medication, ask about **supply arrangements** or prescriptions your home doctor can continue.
- Carry medications in **original packaging** with a translated list (for customs).

## Follow-up
- Ask for a **follow-up schedule** and what to check when you’re home.
- Request an **English discharge summary and results** to share with your home doctor.
- Many hospitals offer **teleconsultation follow-up** after you return.

## Sending results home
- Ask for **digital copies** (PDF) of reports, imaging, and results to keep and share.
- Keep a **central folder** (paper + digital) of everything — diagnosis, treatment, medications, and follow-up.
