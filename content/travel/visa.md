Getting the right visa is the first step. Here’s how it works for a medical visit to China.

## Which visa you need

For a short medical visit (consultation, treatment, or surgery), most patients enter on a **tourist (L) visa** or, for longer treatment/stays, a **medical (M or relevant) visa**. The exact type depends on:
- **How long** you’ll be in China (a few days vs. several weeks)
- **The nature** of the treatment
- **Your nationality** (visa-free / visa-on-arrival rules differ by country)

> ⚠️ **Check current rules before you book.** Visa policies change, and some nationalities have visa-free or transit-visa-free options for short stays. Confirm with the **Chinese embassy/consulate** in your country.

## What you’ll typically need
- A **valid passport** (usually 6+ months beyond your return date)
- A **visa application** with a photo and completed form
- A **letter of invitation** or **medical appointment confirmation** from the hospital/agency (we can provide)
- **Proof of accommodation** and a return flight (often required)
- The **visa fee**

## Timeline
- Standard processing takes **a few business days to two weeks**, depending on the consulate.
- **Start early** — book flights and accommodation after your visa is approved.
