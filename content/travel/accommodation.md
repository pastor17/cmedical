Where you stay during treatment is a big part of comfort. Here are your options.

## Your options

- **Hospital international wards / VIP wards** — private rooms with hospital-standard care, often with English support. Convenient when treatment is daily.
- **Hotels near the hospital** — the most common choice. A hotel within walking distance or a short ride lets you rest comfortably and travel easily for visits.
- **Serviced apartments** — for longer treatment (several weeks), a serviced apartment gives you a kitchen and space.

## How to choose
- **Proximity** — stay within ~30 minutes of the hospital.
- **Type of treatment** — daily treatment favors being close; a one-off procedure favors comfort.
- **Budget** — international hotels are more expensive; there are quality mid-range options nearby.
- **Length of stay** — longer stays often make a serviced apartment better value.
