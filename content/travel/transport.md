Getting around China is easy and affordable. Here’s how.

## Your options

- **Ride-hailing (DiDi):** the most convenient — app-based, reliable, and reasonably priced. We can arrange rides in advance.
- **Metro/subway:** cheap and efficient in all major cities; maps increasingly have English.
- **Taxi:** widely available; ask your hotel to book, or use a ride-hailing app with an English interface.
- **Private car / transfer:** for comfort or mobility needs, we can arrange a dedicated driver for your stay.

## Mobility needs
- If you need **wheelchair-accessible transport** or help with movement (e.g., after surgery), tell us — we can arrange appropriate vehicles and assistance.
- Major hospitals and hotels are **wheelchair-accessible**.

> 💡 **We help:** We coordinate all your city transport, including accessible vehicles, so you can focus on recovery.
