Beyond the hospital bill, you’ll pay for hotels, food, and transport. Here’s how payments work.

## Your payment options

- **Alipay / WeChat Pay (mobile):** the dominant payment method in China. Most places accept it, and both apps now support **linking foreign credit/debit cards**.
- **International cards (Visa/Mastercard):** accepted at hotels, many restaurants, and the international hospital departments — but less universal than mobile pay.
- **Cash (RMB):** still useful as a backup; exchange some at the airport or a bank.

## Practical tips
- **Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay** before you arrive and link your card — it makes daily spending easy.
- **Carry some cash** for small vendors that may not take cards.
- **Keep receipts** — useful for insurance reimbursement (see our insurance guide).
