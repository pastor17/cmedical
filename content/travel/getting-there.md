China’s major medical cities are well connected by international flight. Here’s how to get there.

## International airports by city

| City | Main airport | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Beijing | Beijing Capital (PEK) / Daxing (PKX) | Two airports; check which your flight uses |
| Shanghai | Pudong (PVG) / Hongqiao (SHA) | PVG is the main international one |
| Guangzhou | Baiyun (CAN) | One major international airport |
| Shenzhen | Bao’an (SZX) | Close to the Hong Kong border |
| Hangzhou | Xiaoshan (HGH) | City’s main airport |
| Chengdu | Tianfu (TFU) / Shuangliu (CTU) | Two airports |

## Flight times (typical, by region)
- **From East Asia (Japan/Korea/Singapore):** 2–5 hours
- **From South Asia (India):** 3–6 hours
- **From the Middle East / East Africa:** 6–10 hours
- **From Europe / West Africa:** 10–14 hours
- **From the Americas / Australia:** 12–20+ hours (often one stop)

> Tip: **One-stop** flights are common and usually the best value from Europe, the Americas, and Australia.

## From airport to hospital
- **Airport express / metro:** many cities have a fast, cheap line to the city center.
- **Taxi / ride-hailing (DiDi):** reliable and reasonably priced; use an English app or ask your hotel to book.
- **We can arrange transfer:** for a seamless arrival, we can coordinate a pickup to your hotel or directly to the hospital.
