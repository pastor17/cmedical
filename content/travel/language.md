Language is one of the biggest concerns for international patients. The good news: China’s international departments make this very manageable.

## Interpreter services
- **Hospital international departments** provide professional medical interpreters (English, and often other languages) for consultations.
- We can also arrange a **dedicated medical interpreter** for your whole visit — from the first consultation through discharge.

## Translating your records
- Many hospitals can provide **English versions** of reports, results, and discharge summaries.
- Bring your **prior medical records** — we can help translate key documents into Chinese for your doctors (and provide English copies for your home doctor).

## Practical communication tips
- Use the **international department** — staff there are accustomed to working in English.
- Ask for **written summaries** of your diagnosis and treatment plan (in English).
- For complex cases, ask for a **multidisciplinary (MDT) review** with the interpreter present.

> 💡 **We help:** We coordinate interpreters and record translation so you never miss what’s being said.
