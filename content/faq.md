Here are answers to the questions international patients ask us most. If yours isn't here, [contact us](/cmedical/contact/) — we're happy to help.

## Visas & entry

**Do I need a visa to come to China for treatment?**
Usually yes, but rules depend on your nationality and how long you'll stay. Some countries have visa-free or transit-visa-free options for short stays. **Check with the Chinese embassy/consulate** and see our [Visa guide](/cmedical/travel/visa/).

**How long should I stay?**
It depends on your treatment. A consultation may take 2–4 days; a procedure or surgery often 1–3 weeks including recovery. We'll help you plan the right length.

## The treatment

**Will there be a language barrier?**
China's international departments provide **English-language support and medical interpreters**. We can arrange a dedicated interpreter for your whole visit. See our [Language guide](/cmedical/travel/language/).

**Are Chinese hospitals safe and well-equipped?**
Major Grade III-A hospitals (the top tier) use modern equipment and follow international standards. We profile the top hospitals and their specialties so you can choose well.

**How is the quality of care?**
China has some of the highest-volume, most experienced specialists in the world, especially in procedures like cataract surgery, joint replacement, and cardiac intervention. Quality is high at leading centers.

**Will my doctor speak English?**
International-department staff and the interpreters we arrange communicate in English. Not every doctor does, which is exactly why we coordinate interpreters.

## Cost & payment

**How much will it cost?**
It varies by treatment, hospital, and city. We provide **indicative US$ ranges** for common treatments and a [Cost Estimator](/cmedical/estimator/). Always get a **written, itemized quote** from the hospital.

**Is it cheaper than in my country?**
For many procedures, yes — China is often **substantially less** than the US/UK for comparable care. See our [cost guides](/cmedical/cost/).

**How do I pay?**
International departments typically accept **card or mobile payment** (Alipay/WeChat Pay with a linked foreign card). We guide you on setting this up. See our [Payments guide](/cmedical/travel/payments-travel/).

**Do hospitals work with my insurance?**
Some large international insurers have **direct-billing** arrangements in China; otherwise you pay and claim reimbursement. See our [Insurance guide](/cmedical/insurance/).

## Practical

**Where should I stay?**
Most patients stay in a **hotel near the hospital** or a hospital international ward. We can recommend vetted options. See our [Accommodation guide](/cmedical/travel/accommodation/).

**Can you help with the logistics?**
Yes — we coordinate **visa documents, bookings, interpreter, transport, and accommodation** as a one-stop service. [Start here](/cmedical/contact/).

**What if I have an emergency?**
In a medical emergency, **call local emergency services (120)**. See our [Emergency guide](/cmedical/emergency/).

**Is TCM safe to try alongside my treatment?**
TCM can be a useful complement, but **serious conditions need modern care**. Only use herbs/therapies under a qualified practitioner and tell your doctors. See our [TCM section](/cmedical/tcm/).
