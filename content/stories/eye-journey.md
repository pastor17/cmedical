I’m a 58-year-old from Cape Town with cataracts in both eyes. I chose to have the surgery in Shanghai.

## Why we chose China
- **Volume and skill** — Shanghai’s eye centers perform enormous numbers of cataract surgeries.
- **Cost** — the all-in cost was a small fraction of what I’d understood the same treatment to cost in South Africa.
- **Efficiency** — the process was quick and well organized.

## What actually happened
- **Eye exam** — a pre-op examination, including refraction and imaging.
- **Surgery** — each eye took just minutes, with surface anesthesia. I was home the same day.
- **Follow-up** — a couple of follow-up visits to check healing.

## Cost and communication
The whole thing was a **fraction** of what I expected, and the **international service** handled the English communication. I got a **written quote** before I flew.

## What I’d tell others
- **It’s quick and efficient** — you don’t need to stay long.
- **Get a written quote** and confirm the lens type.
- **Plan a couple of follow-up visits** after the surgery.

My vision is clear again.
