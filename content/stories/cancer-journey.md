*Name changed for privacy. This is a representative patient story, not a guarantee of outcomes or cost.*

I’m a 58-year-old from Toronto, diagnosed with lung cancer. I wanted a **second opinion** and access to newer treatment options, so I came to Beijing.

## How I approached it
I started by shortlisting **comprehensive cancer centers** in Beijing with a multidisciplinary team (MDT). I sent my **pathology and staging** for a remote review before deciding to travel.

## What actually happened
- **Remote consult:** The oncology team reviewed my records and proposed an **MDT discussion**.
- **On site:** I had updated scans and a tissue review, and the team confirmed a **targeted-therapy** approach based on my tumor’s markers.
- **Treatment:** I began the plan in China, with a clear schedule.

## Cost and communication
The cost was **considerably less** than I understood the same care to cost in Canada, and the **international department** provided English support throughout. Getting a **written plan and quote** upfront was what gave me the confidence to come.

## What I’d tell others
- Get a **second opinion** and an **MDT review** for complex cases.
- Bring your **full pathology and staging**.
- **Get a written plan and quote** before you travel.
- Confirm the **follow-up** plan for when you’re back home.

I’m continuing treatment and feeling more in control of the process.

> ⚠️ Cancer care is deeply individual. This is a personal account. Treatment decisions must be made with your treating oncology team based on your specific diagnosis.
