I’m a 67-year-old from Wellington with painful knees. After considering options, I came to Chengdu for a bilateral knee replacement.

## Why we chose China
- **Cost and value** — the total was well below what I understood the same surgery to cost in New Zealand.
- **Experience** — the orthopedic team at the hospital performs very high volumes of joint replacement.
- **Physiotherapy** — early, structured physio was built into the program, which I’d read is key to a good result.

## What actually happened
- **Remote consult** — I sent my X-rays and got a plan and quote first.
- **In Chengdu:** pre-op workup, both knee replacements, and a hospital stay with daily physiotherapy.
- **Recovery:** physio continued after discharge; I was walking comfortably within weeks.

## What I’d tell others
- **Recovery is won in physio** — don’t skip it.
- **Do a remote consult** so you know the plan and cost.
- **Allow time** for recovery before you fly home.

I’m back on my feet and much more active.

> ⚠️ Outcomes vary by person and condition. This is a personal account. Make decisions with your treating orthopedic team.
