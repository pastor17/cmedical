*Name changed for privacy. This is a representative patient story, not a guarantee of outcomes or cost.*

I’m a 61-year-old from Dubai with blockages in three arteries. I was advised I needed a bypass. I researched options and decided to come to Shanghai.

## Why we chose China
- **Cost** — the all-in cost was a **fraction** of the quotes I’d had in the Gulf and Europe.
- **Volume** — the heart center performs a very high number of bypasses, which I found reassuring.
- **International department** — English support made the process manageable.

## What actually happened
- **Remote review first** — I sent my angiogram and got a **written plan and quote** before flying. That’s what gave me the confidence.
- **In Shanghai:** pre-op workup, the bypass surgery, and a hospital stay of about a week with physiotherapy.
- **Recovery:** I was back to daily life within a few weeks.

## What I’d tell others
- **Do a remote consult first** — get the plan and cost before you travel.
- **Choose a high-volume heart center** and ask direct questions.
- **Get a written quote** and know what’s included.

I’m much better now and feel we made the right decision.

> ⚠️ Heart care is time-sensitive and individual. This is a personal account. Make decisions with your treating cardiology team, and in an emergency call local emergency services.
