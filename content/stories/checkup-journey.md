*Name changed for privacy. This is a representative patient story, not a guarantee of outcomes or cost.*

I’m a 45-year-old based in Dubai who wanted a thorough **full-body health screening** before a big work phase. I chose a comprehensive checkup in Guangzhou.

## What I did
I chose a Grade III-A hospital with an **international medical checkup center** and booked a **comprehensive executive package**.

## What it involved
- **One day** on site — fasting in the morning, then the tests.
- **Blood work, imaging (CT + ultrasound), ECG**, and cancer markers.
- **Results** — I got a written report, and the hospital offered an **English version**.
- A short **review with a doctor** to go over the findings.

## Cost and communication
The whole thing cost **well under** the comparable full-body packages I’d seen in the Gulf. The **international center** handled the English communication, and the one-day format was efficient.

## What I’d tell others
- **Fast before** the visit (for the blood work).
- Ask for an **English report** and a doctor’s review.
- **Follow up** on any abnormal results.
- It’s a great way to **baseline** your health, especially before a demanding period.

It was efficient, thorough, and great value.

> ⚠️ Health checkups screen for conditions but are not a substitute for medical care when symptoms are present. This is a personal account.
