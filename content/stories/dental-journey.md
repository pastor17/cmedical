*Name changed for privacy. This is a representative patient story, not a guarantee of outcomes or cost.*

I’m a 54-year-old from Texas. My dentist quoted me around **$28,000** for six implants and crowns. After some research, I decided to try China.

## How I found the hospital

I started by shortlisting Shanghai hospitals with an **international department**, then emailed them my X-rays. One responded in English within a day with a **written quote** — that’s what finally made me feel safe enough to book.

## What actually happened

- **Trip 1 (2 weeks):** Consultation, CT scan, and placement of all six implants. I used a hospital **interpreter** for the consultation — worth every bit. I paid via **Alipay with my Visa card**.
- **Healing period (3 months):** I went home. The clinic checked in by email and adjusted my plan.
- **Trip 2 (4 days):** Abutments and crowns placed. Final follow-up and adjustment.

## Cost and communication

The all-in cost was **well under half** of my US quote. The main “pain points” were the same as anywhere: scheduling and making sure I understood exactly what each visit included. Writing down my questions beforehand helped a lot.

## What I’d tell others

- **Get a written, itemized quote** before you fly.
- Use the **international department** — it made communication much easier.
- Bring a **question list** to every visit.
- Budget for **two trips** if you need implants that require healing time.

It wasn’t without a learning curve, but it was completely doable, and the value was real.
