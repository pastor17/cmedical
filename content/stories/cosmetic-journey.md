*Name changed for privacy. This is a representative patient story, not a guarantee of outcomes or cost.*

I’m a 34-year-old from Sydney who wanted a rhinoplasty. I researched options and decided to come to Shanghai.

## Choosing the surgeon
I was cautious — cosmetic surgery carries real risk. I chose an **accredited hospital with a strong plastic surgery department** and checked the surgeon’s **credentials and case numbers** for rhinoplasty specifically. I asked to see **before/after examples** and got a written plan.

## What happened
- **Consultation:** We discussed my goals, the risks, and the realistic outcome. I got a **written quote**.
- **Pre-op workup:** Bloods and an anesthesia assessment.
- **Surgery:** The procedure itself, under general anesthesia. I stayed in hospital a couple of days.
- **Recovery:** I waited about a week before flying home so swelling could settle a bit.

## Cost and communication
The all-in cost was a **fraction** of the quotes I’d gotten in Australia. I used the **international department** for English support, which helped a lot.

## What I’d tell others
- **Safety first:** choose accredited providers and verify the surgeon’s specific experience.
- **Get a written, itemized quote** and understand the revision policy.
- **Allow recovery time** before you fly home.
- **Manage expectations** — discuss realistic outcomes, not just the “ideal” result.

I’m happy with the result and the process.

> ⚠️ Cosmetic surgery carries real risks, and outcomes vary. This is a personal account. Always choose accredited providers and discuss risks with a qualified surgeon.
