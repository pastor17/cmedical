If you have mobility, sensory, or other special needs, planning ahead makes your trip smoother. Here's what to arrange.

## Tell us your needs early

The more we know, the better we can arrange. Let us know about:
- **Mobility** — wheelchair, walker, or limited mobility
- **Sensory** — hearing or vision needs
- **Communication** — language, hearing aids, interpreters
- **Medical** — conditions that affect travel or treatment
- **Any other accommodations**

## Mobility & transport

- **Wheelchair-accessible transport** — we can arrange accessible vehicles for airport and city transfers.
- **Hospital access** — major Grade III-A hospitals are wheelchair-accessible (ramps, lifts, accessible facilities).
- **Accommodation** — we can arrange **accessible rooms** (ground floor, step-free, accessible bathroom).
- **Airports** — major airports offer wheelchair assistance; request it when booking.

## Communication

- **Interpreters** — we arrange medical interpreters (English and other languages).
- **Translated records** — we translate key documents into Chinese and provide English copies.
- **Medical ID** — we prepare a card in Chinese with your key medical info.

## During treatment

- **Anesthesia** — tell the team about any conditions affecting anesthesia.
- **Recovery** — we arrange help with mobility and daily needs.
- **Follow-up** — we coordinate follow-up (including remote where possible).

## Accommodation

- **Accessible hotels/apartments** near the hospital.
- **Step-free access** and **accessible bathrooms**.
- **Ground-floor or lift access** where needed.

## Tips

- **Plan extra time** — accessibility may require extra time for transfers and appointments.
- **Bring your own aids** if you have preferred equipment (walkers, supports), though hospitals can provide.
- **Carry a medical summary** and **emergency contacts**.
