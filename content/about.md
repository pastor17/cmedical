**中医之道 (The Way of Chinese Medicine)** is a plain-English, educational introduction to Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM).

Our goal is simple: to help curious readers understand what TCM *actually* is — its practices, its ideas, and how it’s used today — without the myths, hype, or jargon.

## What this site is

- A **neutral, educational** overview of TCM practices and concepts.
- Written for **readers who are new to TCM**, in plain English.
- A reference for **how TCM is used alongside modern medicine** in China.

## What this site is not

- Not a **medical provider** or a place to diagnose or treat you.
- Not a source of **medical advice** — for your health, always talk to a qualified clinician.
- Not an endorsement of any specific practitioner or product.

> **Disclaimer:** This site is for general education only. TCM outcomes vary by person and condition. Serious or acute medical conditions should be evaluated with modern diagnostics and treatment. Some herbal products can interact with medications — tell your treating team about anything you take.
