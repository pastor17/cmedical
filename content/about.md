ChinaCare is an English-first guide to getting medical care in China. We help international patients understand the process — from choosing the right hospital, to booking, consulting, paying, and planning follow-up — in plain, honest language.

## Why China?

China is a growing destination for international medical care because of:

- **Lower cost** — comparable care at a fraction of Western prices, especially for dental, cosmetic, IVF, orthopedic, and cardiac procedures.
- **Advanced technology** — CAR-T, proton therapy, robotic surgery, and high-volume specialist teams.
- **Shorter waits** — be seen in weeks rather than months.
- **A unique blend** — modern medicine alongside a long tradition of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM).

## How we help

1. **Clear information** — verified hospital profiles, cost guides, and step-by-step journey guides.
2. **Practical tools** — a cost estimator, checklists, and plain-English explanations.
3. **Honest guidance** — we help you find the *right* hospital for your case, not just the cheapest.

## Our limits

- We are an **information and coordination** resource, not a hospital or a medical provider.
- We do **not** give medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment.
- We do **not** provide emergency care — in an emergency, call local emergency services (120 in China).
