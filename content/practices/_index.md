Traditional Chinese Medicine is a family of related practices. This page introduces each one in plain English — what it is, what it’s used for, and what a session typically feels like.

> ⚠️ **A note on scope:** TCM is best understood as **complementary** to modern medicine. Serious or acute conditions should be diagnosed and managed with modern care. TCM is most commonly used for chronic, functional, and rehabilitation concerns.
