TCM diagnosis (辨证, “discriminating patterns”) is how a practitioner understands your condition before treating it.

## The core idea
Rather than focusing on a single symptom, the practitioner looks at the **whole picture** — a *pattern* of symptoms, your constitution, and how the body is responding.

## What they may look at
- **Pulse** — the feel of the pulse at several points
- **Tongue** — color, shape, and coating
- **History & questions** — sleep, digestion, energy, temperature preference, and more
- **Observation** — posture, complexion, movement

## Why it matters
The treatment is chosen to address the *pattern*, not just the surface symptom. This is why two people with the same modern diagnosis might receive different TCM treatments.

> 💡 TCM diagnosis is complementary to — not a replacement for — modern diagnosis. Serious or new conditions should be evaluated with modern tests.
