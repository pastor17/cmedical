Cupping (拔罐) involves placing small cups on the skin to create gentle suction.

## What it is
Cups (traditionally made of bamboo or glass, now often plastic) are placed on the skin and a mild vacuum is created. They may be left in place or moved (glided) across the area.

## What it’s commonly used for
- Muscle tension and certain pain types
- Easing circulation
- As part of broader pain and rehabilitation plans

## What to expect
You may see circular marks (bruising-like) where the cups sat — these are usually temporary and fade over days to a couple of weeks. This is a normal part of the practice, not a sign of injury.

> 💡 Cupping is generally used for **chronic and functional** concerns. Get modern care first for acute or serious conditions.
