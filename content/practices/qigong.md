Qigong (气功) is a family of gentle practices combining movement, posture, breathing, and meditation.

## What it is
Practitioners move slowly through a set of postures and gestures, coordinated with breathing and focused attention. There are standing, sitting, and moving forms.

## What it’s commonly used for
- Gentle exercise and mobility, especially for older adults
- Stress reduction and relaxation
- Supporting rehabilitation and recovery
- General well-being and balance

## What a session is like
A session is calm and unhurried — slow, deliberate movements with focused breathing. It’s accessible to most people and often practiced as daily self-care.

> 💡 Qigong is a gentle practice; it complements rather than replaces medical care.
