Tuina (推拿) is a form of therapeutic massage and manual manipulation. The word is often translated as “push and grasp.”

## What it is
A practitioner uses hands, fingers, palms, and sometimes forearms to apply pressure and manipulate muscles, joints, and specific points. It’s closely related to, and often grouped with, massage therapy.

## What it’s commonly used for
- Musculoskeletal tension and pain (back, neck, shoulders)
- Postural issues
- Supporting mobility and recovery
- General relaxation

## What a session is like
A session involves focused pressure and manipulation of the affected areas, often combined with joint mobilization. People commonly describe it as deep, therapeutic, and relaxing.

> 💡 For acute injury or suspected fracture, get a modern medical evaluation first.
