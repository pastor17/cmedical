Dietary therapy (食疗) uses food as a way to support health, balance, and recovery — the idea that “food and medicine have the same origin.”

## What it is
Rather than treating a single symptom, dietary therapy suggests food choices aligned with a person’s condition and constitution. It’s about *what* and *how* you eat as part of your overall care.

## Common approaches
- Warming foods for conditions associated with “cold”
- Cooling foods for “heat”-type imbalances
- Gentle, easily digested foods during recovery
- Seasonal eating aligned with the time of year

> 💡 Dietary therapy is best seen as **supportive and complementary** — a way to reinforce your overall health, not a substitute for medical treatment of serious conditions.
