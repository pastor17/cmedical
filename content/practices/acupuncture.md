Acupuncture is the most recognized form of Traditional Chinese Medicine. It involves placing very thin, sterile needles at specific **acupoints** on the body.

## What it is
A practitioner inserts fine needles into specific points. They’re left in place for a short period — typically 20–40 minutes. Most people describe mild pressure, tingling, or warmth rather than sharp pain.

## What it’s commonly used for
- **Pain** — back, neck, knee, headache, post-operative pain
- **Nausea** — including nausea associated with chemotherapy
- **Rehabilitation** — supporting recovery after illness or surgery
- **Stress and sleep** — as part of a broader plan

Evidence and experience vary by condition; acupuncture is most often considered for **chronic and functional** concerns rather than acute emergencies.

## What a session is like
1. Consultation and assessment.
2. Needles placed (sterile, single-use), then left for 20–40 minutes.
3. A course of sessions is typical for many conditions.

> 💡 If you take medication or have a bleeding or immune condition, tell your practitioner. Acupuncture is usually a **course of treatments**, not a one-off fix.
