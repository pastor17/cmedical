Moxibustion (艾灸) is a warming therapy that involves burning **mugwort** (a dried herb) near or on acupoints.

## What it is
The smoke and warmth from burning mugwort are directed at specific points on the body. There are different techniques — from indirect (held above the skin) to more direct approaches.

## What it’s commonly used for
- Certain pain types, especially those associated with “cold” in TCM terms
- Circulation-related complaints
- As a complement to other TCM practices

## What to expect
You feel a gentle warmth at the treatment area. It’s generally relaxing. Because it involves heat, it’s done carefully by a qualified practitioner.

> 💡 Moxibustion is typically part of a broader treatment plan, not a stand-alone cure.
