Herbal medicine (中药) uses formulas made from plants, minerals, and sometimes animal-derived ingredients, tailored to an individual’s condition.

## What it is
A TCM practitioner composes a **formula** — a combination of ingredients in specific proportions — based on your pattern of symptoms. Formulas are often individualized and adjusted as your condition changes.

## What it’s commonly used for
- Digestive and functional complaints
- Supporting recovery from illness
- Chronic conditions as a complement to modern care

## Important cautions
- **Interactions:** Some herbal products can interact with prescription medications (for example, affecting blood thinners). **Always tell your main treating team what you’re taking.**
- **Quality & safety:** Choose reputable sources and qualified practitioners.
- **Not a replacement** for modern diagnosis and treatment of serious conditions.

> 💡 Think of herbal medicine as one tool in a broader plan — best coordinated with the rest of your care.
