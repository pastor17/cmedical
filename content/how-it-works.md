Here's how our one-stop service works, from your first message to aftercare when you're home.

## Step 1 — Start a consultation
[Send your records and details](/cmedical/contact/). Tell us your condition, what you're hoping for, and your timeline.

## Step 2 — We assess & recommend
We review your case and recommend **suitable hospitals and specialists** for your condition, based on their strengths.

## Step 3 — Get a plan & written quote
We help arrange a consultation and obtain a **written, itemized treatment plan and cost quote** — so you know exactly what to expect.

## Step 4 — We coordinate your trip
We arrange the practical side:
- **Visa** documents (hospital invitation/confirmation)
- **Interpreter** for your consultations
- **Transport** (airport and city)
- **Accommodation** near the hospital

## Step 5 — Your treatment
You receive care at the hospital, with **English support** throughout. We're available to help coordinate anything that comes up.

## Step 6 — Aftercare & follow-up
Before you leave, we help set up:
- **Medication continuity** and a translated list
- **Follow-up schedule** and English records for your home doctor
- **Teleconsultation** follow-up after you return

> 💡 **One point of contact for all of it.** [Start a consultation](/cmedical/contact/) and we handle the coordination.
