Your privacy — especially your **medical information** — is serious. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

## Information you may share

When you contact us (e.g., for a remote consultation), you may share:
- Contact details (name, email, country, phone)
- Medical information (records, imaging, lab results, diagnosis)

## How we use it

- To **connect you with the right hospital or provider** for your case.
- To **respond to your questions** and coordinate logistics.
- We do **not** sell your information.
- We do **not** share your medical records with third parties **without your consent**, except where necessary to complete a service you explicitly requested.

## Data handling

- Medical information is treated as **sensitive and confidential**.
- We aim to **minimize retention** — holding data only as long as needed to complete your request.
- If you send records over email, be aware that email is not always fully secure. For large or sensitive files, we can arrange a more secure transfer on request.

## Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR or similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
