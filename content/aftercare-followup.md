The trip isn't over when you fly home — **aftercare is where your recovery really continues**. Here's how to make sure you leave China with everything you need.

## Collect these documents before you leave
Ask the hospital (or your coordinator) to give you **official copies** of:
- **Discharge summary (出院小结)** — the single most important document
- **Diagnosis & procedure report(s)**
- **All lab results and imaging** (on a **USB / CD** if possible, plus printed copies)
- **Operative / surgical report** if you had surgery
- **Medication list** — names, doses, and duration
- **Official invoice (发票) and itemized bill** — for your insurance claim
- **Referral / follow-up plan** — what to do next and when

> 💡 **Request English translations** of the key documents (discharge summary, diagnosis, med list). Some international departments provide this; if not, a certified translation helps your home doctor.

## Prescriptions & medication
- Ask whether your **prescription can be issued** so you can fill it — and whether any meds are **restricted** (some can't be carried across borders).
- Carry your **medication list + doctor's note** explaining what each drug is and why (for customs).
- Confirm **refill availability** back home, especially for specialty oncology or cardiac drugs that may differ by country.
- For **controlled/restricted substances**, check the rules of your **home country and transit countries** before you fly.

## Setting up follow-up
- **Book your follow-up before you leave** — either a return visit in China or a tele-consultation.
- **Ask your home doctor** to coordinate: send them the discharge summary and results in advance.
- For **remote follow-up**, ChinaCare can help set up a **tele-consultation** with your treating team (see [Tele-consultation](/cmedical/teleconsultation/)).
- Set a **calendar reminder** for key post-op or post-chemo checkpoints.

## Practical post-trip checklist
- [ ] Discharge summary + diagnosis (English copy)
- [ ] All results + imaging (USB/CD + print)
- [ ] Med list + doctor's note for customs
- [ ] Invoice (发票) + itemized bill for insurance
- [ ] Follow-up appointment booked
- [ ] Results shared with your home doctor
- [ ] Insurance claim filed (see [Insurance & claims](/cmedical/insurance-claims-howto/))

## Red flags that need prompt attention
Fever, worsening pain, unusual swelling/drainage, or symptoms that return after treatment — **don't wait** for the scheduled follow-up. Contact your treating team or see a doctor promptly.

> ⚠️ **Verify before you travel.** Document availability and translation services vary by hospital. Request your full file and translations directly before you leave.
