Ready to take a step toward getting care in China? The fastest way is a **remote consultation**: send your medical records and we’ll connect you with the right hospital team for a plan and a written quote — before you book any flights.

## How it works

<ol>
  <li><strong>Tell us what you need.</strong> Share your condition, the treatment you’re considering, and your preferred city/timeline.</li>
  <li><strong>Send your records.</strong> Past medical records, imaging (DICOM/CD), lab results, and a list of current medications help us match you with the right team.</li>
  <li><strong>Get a plan and quote.</strong> We help connect you with the hospital’s international department to receive a treatment plan and cost estimate.</li>
  <li><strong>Decide and travel.</strong> Once you’re comfortable, we can help with booking, visa guidance, and logistics.</li>
</ol>

## What to include in your first message

- Your country and preferred language
- The condition or treatment you’re seeking
- Preferred hospital city (if any) and rough timeline
- Key medical documents (attach or link)

> ⚠️ **Privacy:** Your medical information is confidential. See our [Privacy Policy](/cmedical/privacy/). We do not share your records without your consent, and we do not provide emergency care.

> 💡 **A note on honesty:** We help you find the *right* hospital for your case — not just the cheapest or the most profitable. If a treatment in China isn’t the best fit for you, we’ll tell you.
