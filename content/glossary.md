A quick reference of common medical terms and their Chinese equivalents. This helps you understand your records and communicate at the hospital.

## Common conditions & symptoms

| English | 中文 (Chinese) |
|---------|----------------|
| Pain | 疼痛 (téng tòng) |
| Fever | 发烧 (fā shāo) |
| Headache | 头痛 (tóu tòng) |
| Cough | 咳嗽 (ké sou) |
| Nausea | 恶心 (ě xīn) |
| Dizziness | 头晕 (tóu yūn) |
| Fatigue | 疲劳 (pí láo) |
| Allergy | 过敏 (guò mǐn) |

## Useful phrases

| English | 中文 (Chinese) |
|---------|----------------|
| I need help | 我需要帮助 (wǒ xū yào bāng zhù) |
| Where is the emergency room? | 急诊在哪里? (jí zhěn zài nǎ lǐ?) |
| I'm allergic to penicillin | 我对青霉素过敏 (wǒ duì qīng méi sù guò mǐn) |
| How much is it? | 多少钱? (duō shao qián?) |
| Please call my doctor | 请打给我医生 (qǐng dǎ gěi wǒ yī shēng) |
