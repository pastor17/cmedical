Treatment is only part of planning a medical trip to China. The **practical and support details** are just as important — and getting them right is what makes the whole experience comfortable. Here’s the full picture, in one place.

## Your complete checklist

### 1. Before you book
- ✅ **Visa** — get the right visa; start early ([Visa & Entry](/cmedical/travel/visa/))
- ✅ **Insurance** — confirm your coverage and claim route ([Insurance & Claims](/cmedical/insurance/))
- ✅ **Budget** — plan for treatment + travel + stay + living ([Costs](/cmedical/cost/))
- ✅ **Medical records** — gather and translate prior records

### 2. Getting there
- ✅ **Flights & airport** — plan the route and airport transfer ([Flights & Getting There](/cmedical/travel/getting-there/))
- ✅ **Arrival transfer** — airport to hotel/hospital

### 3. While you’re here
- ✅ **Language & interpreter** — communicate clearly ([Language & Interpreters](/cmedical/travel/language/))
- ✅ **Where to stay** — hospital ward, hotel, or apartment ([Accommodation](/cmedical/travel/accommodation/))
- ✅ **Food & diet** — eat well, manage dietary needs ([Food & Diet](/cmedical/travel/food/))
- ✅ **Getting around** — transport and mobility ([Transport](/cmedical/travel/transport/))
- ✅ **Payments** — cards, cash, mobile pay ([Payments](/cmedical/travel/payments-travel/))

### 4. After you return home
- ✅ **Medication continuity** — keep taking the right medicine ([Aftercare](/cmedical/travel/aftercare/))
- ✅ **Follow-up** — schedule checks, share results with your home doctor
- ✅ **Teleconsultation** — remote follow-up if needed
