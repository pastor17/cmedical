**Qi** (气, often pronounced “chee”) is a core concept in TCM — the vital energy or life force that animates the body.

## What Qi is
Qi is the subtle energy that keeps the body functioning. It flows through pathways called **meridians** (经络) that connect the organs and surface of the body. Practices like acupuncture work with the idea of moving or balancing Qi along these pathways.

## Related terms
- **Qi deficiency** — when energy is low, leading to fatigue or weakness
- **Qi stagnation** — when flow is blocked, associated with certain kinds of pain
- **Meridians** — the pathways along which Qi is thought to travel

> 💡 Qi is a *model* concept — a way TCM describes the body’s functioning, not a substance you can measure directly. It’s best understood as part of TCM’s explanatory framework.
