The central idea in TCM is that **health is a state of balance** — a dynamic equilibrium between the body’s systems, and between the body and its environment. Illness is understood as a disturbance of that balance.

## What “balance” means here
It’s not a single static state. It’s an ongoing, dynamic process — the body constantly adjusting in response to activity, seasons, emotions, diet, and more. Treatment aims to *restore* and *support* that balance rather than simply suppress a symptom.

## Why it matters
This is why TCM looks at the whole person and their context, not just a lab value. The same symptom in two different people might be understood differently, because the underlying pattern of imbalance differs.

> 💡 Think of it as a different *model* of health — complementary to, not competing with, the biomedical model.
