**Yin and Yang** (阴阳) are complementary opposites — a fundamental idea in TCM and in Chinese philosophy more broadly.

## What they are
Yin and Yang describe qualities that are opposite but interdependent:
- **Yin** — cool, calm, inward, nourishing
- **Yang** — warm, active, outward, driving

Everything contains both. Health is seen as their **dynamic balance**; illness as an excess, deficiency, or separation of one relative to the other.

## How it’s used
A practitioner may describe a condition in terms of yin/yang imbalance — for example, “excess heat” (yang) or “deficient yin” — to guide treatment. It’s a *descriptive framework* for understanding patterns.

> 💡 Yin and yang is a *lens*, not a literal substance. It’s how TCM organizes and describes the body’s states.
