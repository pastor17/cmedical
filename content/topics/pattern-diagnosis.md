**Pattern diagnosis** (辨证) is the process by which a TCM practitioner forms an overall understanding of your condition.

## How it works
Instead of zeroing in on one symptom, the practitioner gathers the **whole picture**:
- Pulse (feel at several points)
- Tongue (color, shape, coating)
- History and detailed questions (sleep, digestion, energy, temperature preference)
- Observation (posture, complexion, movement)

From this they identify a **pattern** — a coherent set of signs that explains the whole presentation. Treatment is then chosen to address that pattern.

## Why it matters
This is why two people with the same modern diagnosis might receive different TCM treatments — their *patterns* differ. It’s the reasoning that links diagnosis to practice.

> 💡 Pattern diagnosis is complementary to modern diagnosis. Serious or new conditions should be evaluated with modern tests.
