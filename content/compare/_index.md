Cost is one of the main reasons patients travel to China. But “cheaper” is too simple — what matters is the **value**: the cost **relative to the quality of care**. This section compares treatment costs in China with your home country, procedure by procedure.

> ⚠️ **These are indicative US$ planning ranges**, not quotes. They help you plan and set expectations. **Always get a written, itemized quote** from the hospital for your specific case. Prices vary by hospital, city, procedure, and complexity, and change over time.

## How to use these comparisons

- Pick your **home country** to see China vs. your cost side by side.
- Treat the numbers as **ballpark** — your actual quote will differ based on your case.
- Combine with our **cost guides** (per-procedure detail) and the **Cost Estimator** for a ballpark.

## What “all-in” usually includes

Surgery fees, anesthesia, hospital stay, tests, and often the implant/device. It may **not** include: travel, accommodation, interpreter, follow-up abroad, or some medications. See [What's in a Quote](/cmedical/cost/whats-included/) for the full anatomy.

## The bigger picture

China’s costs are typically **a fraction** of the US/UK for comparable care at leading Grade III-A hospitals — often **30–80% less**, depending on the procedure. The savings are real, but **quality and safety matter too** — see [Quality & Safety in China](/cmedical/quality-safety/) before you decide.
