Compared with the **UAE/Gulf** private medical system, treatment in China is often **less**, especially for major procedures. (The Gulf has excellent, high-quality private care — but at a premium.) Here are indicative US$ ranges.

> The UAE/Gulf figures are broad private averages; the China figures are at leading Grade III-A hospitals. **Both are planning ranges, not quotes.**

## China vs. UAE / Gulf — indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | China (US$) | UAE/Gulf private (US$) | Typical saving |
|-----------|-------------|------------------------|----------------|
| Cataract surgery (per eye) | 500 – 3,000 | 2,000 – 5,000 | 40–75% |
| LASIK / SMILE | 300 – 2,500 | 2,500 – 5,000 | 60–80% |
| Dental implant (per tooth) | 500 – 1,500 | 1,500 – 4,000 | 50–80% |
| Single coronary stent | 2,000 – 8,000 | 25,000 – 70,000+ | 70–85% |
| Bypass surgery (CABG) | 5,000 – 25,000 | 40,000 – 100,000+ | 80–90% |
| Knee replacement | 8,000 – 20,000 | 15,000 – 40,000 | 40–80% |
| Hip replacement | 9,000 – 22,000 | 20,000 – 50,000 | 50–80% |
| IVF (per cycle, base) | 3,000 – 6,000 | 5,000 – 15,000+ | 40–70% |
| Rhinoplasty | 1,500 – 6,000 | 4,000 – 12,000+ | 50–80% |
| Full-body checkup | 500 – 3,000 | 500 – 5,000 | 0–60% |

> ⚠️ **Indicative planning ranges only.** Actual costs vary by hospital, city, procedure, complexity, and your individual case. **Always get a written, itemized quote** from the hospital. These figures are for planning and are not quotes or guarantees.

## What affects the difference

- **Procedure complexity** — more complex cases cost more everywhere.
- **Hospital and city** — top-tier urban centers (Beijing, Shanghai) cost more than other cities.
- **Implant/device brand** — domestic vs. imported differ significantly.
- **Length of stay** — a longer hospital stay raises the total.
- **Follow-up** — some quotes exclude follow-up visits and medication.

## Reading the savings

The **savings** column is the difference between the top of the UAE / Gulf range and the China range — a rough planning figure, **not** a guaranteed discount. Your actual saving depends on your specific case and quote.
