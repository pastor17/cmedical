Compared with Canada's **private** medical system, treatment in China is often **less**. (Provincial public insurance covers medically necessary care but has long waits for many elective procedures; private care is available but costly.) Here are indicative US$ ranges.

> The Canada private figures are broad averages; the China figures are at leading Grade III-A hospitals. **Both are planning ranges, not quotes.**

## China vs. Canada — indicative US$ ranges

| Procedure | China (US$) | Canada private (US$) | Typical saving |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Cataract surgery (per eye) | 500 – 3,000 | 3,000 – 5,000 | 60–85% |
| LASIK / SMILE | 300 – 2,500 | 3,000 – 5,000 | 70–85% |
| Dental implant (per tooth) | 500 – 1,500 | 3,000 – 5,000 | 70–80% |
| Single coronary stent | 2,000 – 8,000 | 30,000 – 70,000+ | 75–85% |
| Bypass surgery (CABG) | 5,000 – 25,000 | 50,000 – 100,000+ | 80–90% |
| Knee replacement | 8,000 – 20,000 | 25,000 – 45,000 | 60–80% |
| Hip replacement | 9,000 – 22,000 | 30,000 – 50,000 | 70–85% |
| IVF (per cycle, base) | 3,000 – 6,000 | 8,000 – 18,000+ | 60–70% |
| Rhinoplasty | 1,500 – 6,000 | 6,000 – 15,000+ | 70–85% |
| Full-body checkup | 500 – 3,000 | 500 – 4,000 | 30–60% |

> ⚠️ **Indicative planning ranges only.** Actual costs vary by hospital, city, procedure, complexity, and your individual case. **Always get a written, itemized quote** from the hospital. These figures are for planning and are not quotes or guarantees.

## What affects the difference

- **Procedure complexity** — more complex cases cost more everywhere.
- **Hospital and city** — top-tier urban centers (Beijing, Shanghai) cost more than other cities.
- **Implant/device brand** — domestic vs. imported differ significantly.
- **Length of stay** — a longer hospital stay raises the total.
- **Follow-up** — some quotes exclude follow-up visits and medication.

## Reading the savings

The **savings** column is the difference between the top of the Canada range and the China range — a rough planning figure, **not** a guaranteed discount. Your actual saving depends on your specific case and quote.
