Pay-and-reimburse is the most common route: you pay the hospital, then claim the costs back from your insurer.

## Documents you’ll need
- **Itemized invoices** — broken down by service, in Chinese and ideally English
- **Diagnosis and treatment records**
- **Prescriptions and medication lists**
- **Proof of payment** — receipts or card statements
- **Flight and hotel receipts** (if travel is covered)
- Your **insurance claim form** and policy details

## The typical process
1. **Collect** all documents after treatment (we can provide English copies).
2. **Submit** the claim to your insurer (online or by post, depending on the insurer).
3. **Follow up** if the insurer requests more information.
4. **Receive** reimbursement (timing varies by insurer).

## Tips
- **Keep original receipts** — many insurers require them.
- **Submit promptly** — some policies have a time limit for claims.
- **Keep copies** of everything you submit.

> ⚠️ **Requirements vary by insurer and policy.** Check your policy’s claim rules before you travel.
