Here’s how insurance generally works for treatment in China, in plain English.

## Step 1: Check your policy before you travel
- Confirm your policy **covers treatment abroad / in China**.
- Ask whether your insurer has **direct-billing (network) arrangements** with Chinese hospitals.
- Note any **pre-authorization** or **guarantee letter** requirements.

## Step 2: Choose your model
- **Direct billing** (if available): the hospital bills your insurer directly — less cash outlay for you. You may still need a **guarantee letter** from your insurer.
- **Pay-and-reimburse** (most common): you pay the hospital, then submit a claim to your insurer.

## Step 3: Keep every document
- **Itemized invoices** (in Chinese and English)
- **Diagnosis and treatment records**
- **Prescriptions and medication lists**
- **Proof of payment** (receipts / card statements)
- Your **insurance policy details** and claim form

> ⚠️ **Always confirm with your insurer** before you travel. Coverage, direct-billing availability, and documentation requirements vary by insurer and policy.
