Understanding how insurance works for treatment in China removes a lot of stress. This section explains your options and the documents you’ll need.

## The two main models

- **Direct billing:** The hospital bills your insurance/insurance provider directly. This depends on your insurer having a **network agreement** in China — some international insurers do.
- **Pay-and-reimburse:** You pay the hospital, then claim reimbursement from your insurer. This is the most common route.

> ⚠️ **Confirm with your insurer first.** Coverage, direct-billing availability, and requirements vary by insurer and policy. Always check your policy **before** you travel.
