If you don’t have travel or health insurance, you’ll pay for your treatment directly. Here’s how to plan.

## What to plan
- **Budget for the whole trip:** treatment + hospital stay + flights + accommodation + food + transport.
- **Get a written, itemized quote** from the hospital before you travel, so you know the cost.
- **Set up payment methods** (mobile pay with a linked card, plus some cash) — see our payments guide.

## How to pay
- The hospital’s **international department** typically handles payments for foreign patients.
- You can often pay by **card or mobile payment** with a linked foreign card.
- Ask about **deposit** and **final bill** timing.

## Reducing cost
- Compare **costs** between comparable centers (China is often substantially cheaper than the US/UK for similar care).
- Ask what’s **included** in a quote (follow-up, medication, etc.).
- Use the **Cost Estimator** for a ballpark before you travel.

> ⚠️ **Consider travel insurance** even if you don’t have health insurance — it can cover unexpected events and help with repatriation if needed.
