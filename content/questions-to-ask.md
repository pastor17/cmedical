The right questions protect you and make the whole experience smoother. Use this checklist when choosing a hospital, reviewing your case with a doctor, or getting a quote.

> 💡 **Tip:** Ask these **in writing** where possible (email is fine). Written answers are your records.

## About the hospital

1. Is the hospital **Grade III-A (三甲)** and what are its accreditations (e.g., JCI)?
2. Is there a **dedicated center/department** for my condition?
3. How many of **my specific procedure** do you perform per year?
4. Do you have an **international patient department** with English support?
5. What is your **policy for complications** and how would I be cared for?
6. Can I get **written, itemized billing** in English?

## About the doctor

7. How many years of experience do you have in my specific condition/procedure?
8. How many of **this exact procedure** have you personally performed?
9. What are the **typical outcomes / success rates** for my case?
10. What are the **main risks** for my specific case?
11. Will you be the **surgeon/lead** throughout, or will others be involved?
12. Can you share your **credentials, training, and any publications**?

## About the treatment

13. What is my **diagnosis** and how are you confirming it?
14. What are my **treatment options**, including alternatives?
15. What is the **realistic expected outcome** for me?
16. How long will the **hospital stay** be?
17. What is the **recovery timeline** and what can I expect?
18. What **follow-up** will I need after treatment (and can it be done remotely)?
19. What **medications** will I be on, and how do I get them after I leave?
20. Are there **lifestyle or activity restrictions** after the procedure?

## About the quote & payment

21. Is this price **all-inclusive**, or are there separate charges?
22. What is **specifically included** and what is **not**?
23. What is the **implant/device brand** (if applicable) and its cost?
24. Is there a **deposit**, and is it **refundable**?
25. What is the **payment schedule** (when do I pay each part)?
26. What happens to costs if the **procedure is more complex** than planned?
27. Are **follow-up visits and post-discharge medication** included?
28. Can I get the **itemized bill and records in English** for my insurance?

## About logistics

29. Can you help with my **visa** (invitation/confirmation letter)?
30. Is an **interpreter** provided for my consultations?
31. What **transport and accommodation** do you recommend or arrange?
32. What should I **prepare and bring** (records, imaging, medications)?
