中国人民解放军总医院（301医院） (Chinese PLA General Hospital (301 Hospital)) is a leading Grade III-A hospital serving the beijing region. It combines a broad range of strong specialties with teaching and research programs, and provides international patient services for foreign patients.


**A multi-center military flagship.** The PLA General Hospital is organized into several medical centers plus specialized departments, with a **Hainan branch (~720 beds)**. Its cardiology unit runs ~241 beds with 7,000+ surgeries a year, and it operates one of the largest kidney-disease treatment centers in the country.
