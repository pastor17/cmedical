The First Affiliated Hospital of Zhejiang University School of Medicine (浙江大学医学院附属第一医院), founded in 1947, is one of the leading general hospitals in east China. It is a major referral and research center, particularly well known for cardiology, organ transplantation, and oncology.

## Strengths for international patients

- **Cardiology** — a nationally recognized heart center for complex cardiovascular care.
- **Organ transplant** — a leading transplant center (liver, kidney, heart).
- **Oncology & neurosurgery** — comprehensive, high-volume programs.
- **Broad specialty coverage** — comprehensive general and specialty care.

## International service

The hospital offers **international patient services** with English-language support for appointments, interpretation, and coordination.

## Practical notes

- **Booking:** Contact the international patient service by phone or email — the most reliable route for foreigners.
- **Payment:** WeChat Pay / Alipay with a linked foreign card is the common route; confirm with the hospital.
- **Location:** Hangzhou — a major city with Xiaoshan International Airport.
