The First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University (中山大学附属第一医院), founded in 1926, is one of the leading general hospitals in southern China and a major referral, teaching, and research center. It is well known for its breadth of specialties and its integration of modern medicine with Traditional Chinese Medicine.

## Strengths for international patients

- **Oncology** — comprehensive cancer programs with surgery, chemotherapy, and targeted/immunotherapy.
- **Cardiology** — a strong heart center for cardiovascular diagnosis and treatment.
- **Integrative medicine** — a notable example of TCM working alongside modern care, useful for patients open to a combined approach.
- **Health checkups** — comprehensive screening and executive medical checkup programs.

## International service

The hospital provides **international patient services** with English-language support, making it accessible to foreign patients and to visitors from the region.

## Practical notes

- **Booking:** Contact the international patient service by phone or email — the most reliable route for foreigners.
- **Payment:** WeChat Pay / Alipay with a linked foreign card is the common route; confirm specifics with the hospital.
- **Location:** Guangzhou — a gateway to southern China and Southeast Asia, with Baiyun International Airport.
