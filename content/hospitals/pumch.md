Peking Union Medical College Hospital (PUMCH), founded in 1921, is one of the oldest and most respected hospitals in China and a flagship of the national medical system. It is consistently ranked among the top general hospitals in the country and is a key referral center for complex cases.

## Strengths for international patients

- **Oncology** — a major cancer center with strong surgical, chemotherapy, and emerging-cell-therapy programs.
- **Cardiology & cardiac surgery** — high-volume, complex heart procedures.
- **Neurology & neurosurgery** — brain, spine, and neurological conditions.
- **Multidisciplinary teams** — for complex, multi-system cases.

## International service

PUMCH operates an **International Medical Department** serving foreign patients, with English-language support. This is the recommended entry point for international patients: it handles appointments, interpreters, and coordination.

## Practical notes

- **Booking:** Contact the international department by phone or email — the most reliable route for foreigners (the hospital’s app is built primarily for domestic patients).
- **Payment:** WeChat Pay / Alipay with a linked foreign card is accepted at most windows; confirm with the international desk for your case.
- **Location:** Central Beijing; allow time for travel from the airport or your hotel.

> ⚠️ **Verify before you travel.** Phone numbers, email addresses, and service details change. Confirm the international department’s current contact and your case’s specifics directly before booking. Figures and policies shown are indicative and subject to change.
