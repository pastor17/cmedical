West China Hospital (华西医院), founded in 1892 and affiliated with Sichuan University, is the leading general hospital in western China and one of the country’s highest-ranked hospitals. It is a major referral and research center, especially well known for ophthalmology and organ transplantation.

## Strengths for international patients

- **Ophthalmology** — one of the strongest eye centers in China, with advanced surgery for cataract, retina, and refractive conditions.
- **Organ transplant** — a leading transplant center (liver, kidney, and others).
- **Orthopedics & neurosurgery** — high-volume surgical programs.
- **Broad specialty coverage** — comprehensive care across most disciplines.

## International service

West China Hospital offers **international patient services** with English-language support, making it accessible to foreign patients and visitors from the region.

## Practical notes

- **Booking:** Contact the international patient service by phone or email — the most reliable route for foreigners.
- **Payment:** WeChat Pay / Alipay with a linked foreign card is the common route; confirm with the hospital.
- **Location:** Chengdu — a convenient hub for western China and neighboring regions, with a major international airport.
