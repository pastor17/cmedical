Renji Hospital (瑞金医院), founded in 1907 and affiliated with Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s School of Medicine, is one of China’s premier Grade III-A general hospitals. It is a major referral and teaching center and is consistently ranked among the leading hospitals in the country.

## Strengths for international patients

- **Oncology** — a leading cancer hospital with comprehensive treatment programs.
- **Cardiology** — a nationally recognized heart center for complex cardiovascular care.
- **Orthopedics & more** — broad specialty coverage including hematology, endocrinology, and surgical subspecialties.
- **Health checkups** — comprehensive executive and screening programs.

## International service

Renji provides **international patient services** with English-language support for appointments, interpretation, and coordination — a practical entry point for foreign patients.

## Practical notes

- **Booking:** Contact the international patient service directly (phone or email) — simpler for foreigners than the domestic app.
- **Payment:** WeChat Pay / Alipay with a linked foreign card is the common route; confirm specifics with the hospital.
- **Location:** Central Shanghai, well connected to the metro and international transport.
