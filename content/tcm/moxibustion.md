Moxibustion (艾灸, āi jiǔ) is a TCM therapy that uses the heat of a dried herb (mugwort) to warm specific points on the body. Here's what it involves.

## How it works
A cone or stick of dried **mugwort (艾)** is burned near (or on) an acupuncture point to deliver deep warmth. This is believed to warm qi, boost energy, and relieve pain or weakness.

## Types
- **Moxa stick** — a smoldering stick held near the point (most common).
- **Moxa cone** — a small cone placed on a point (often with a protective pad).
- **Indirect moxibustion** — warmth delivered through an object or at a distance.

## What to expect
- A **warm, soothing** sensation (not burning).
- A session is typically 15–30 minutes.
- Often used for **chronic pain, cold conditions, fatigue, and digestive issues**.

## Practical tips
- Tell the practitioner about any **skin sensitivity**.
- It produces a **herbal scent** — it's normal.
- Often combined with **acupuncture**.

> ⚠️ Moxibustion is generally safe when done properly, but has contraindications. Discuss your health history first.
