Herbal medicine is one of the most widely used TCM treatments in China. Here's how it works, in plain English.

> ⚠️ **Herbs are medicine.** They can interact with other medications and aren't safe for everyone. **Always tell your doctors about all medications, including any herbs**, and only use herbs under a qualified practitioner's guidance.

## How it works
A TCM practitioner designs a **formula** — a combination of herbs — tailored to your specific diagnosis (your pattern). Formulas are adjusted as your condition changes.

## Common forms
- **Decoctions (汤)** — dried herbs boiled into a liquid, drunk daily
- **Granules** — pre-extracted powder, mixed with water (convenient)
- **Pills and capsules** — for maintenance or chronic conditions
- **External use** — poultices, washes, or baths

## What to expect
- A typical course is several days to weeks, with follow-up to adjust the formula.
- Formulas are **individualized** — the same condition in two people may get different formulas.
- Prepare and take as instructed; some require specific timing.

## Practical tips for international patients
- Ask for an **English explanation** of your formula and what it's for.
- **Keep a list** of herbs taken (for customs and your home doctor).
- **Confirm interactions** with any Western medication you're on.
