Tuina (推拿, tuī ná) is TCM therapeutic massage, using specific techniques to move qi and blood, release tension, and support the body. Here's what it involves.

## How it works
A practitioner uses **pressing, kneading, rolling, and other hand techniques** on specific points and areas. Unlike a relaxing massage, tuina is **therapeutic** — targeted at a specific condition.

## Common uses
- **Musculoskeletal pain** (back, neck, shoulder, joints)
- **Stiffness and restricted movement**
- **Digestive issues**
- **Rehabilitation** after injury

## What to expect
- A session is typically 20–40 minutes.
- You'll be asked to change position during the session.
- Some **soreness** afterward is possible (like after exercise).

## Practical tips
- Tell the practitioner about **injuries, pain, or conditions**.
- Wear comfortable clothing that allows movement.
- It's often used alongside acupuncture or herbal treatment.

> ⚠️ Tuina is generally safe but has contraindications. Discuss your health history first.
