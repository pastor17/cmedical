Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is a major part of care in China — from acupuncture and herbal medicine to tuina (therapeutic massage) and cupping. This section explains what TCM is, how it’s practiced, and how it can work alongside modern (Western) medicine.

> TCM is best understood as **complementary** to modern medicine, not a replacement. Serious conditions should be evaluated with modern diagnostics. Read our [TCM guide](/cmedical/tcm/what-is-tcm/) to get started.
