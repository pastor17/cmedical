📖 Core ideas to understand

Balance & Harmony

Health as the body’s dynamic balance; illness as imbalance to be restored.

 🔋

Qi (生命能量)

The vital energy that flows through the body along pathways (meridians).

 ☯️

Yin & Yang

Complementary opposites; health is their dynamic balance, not their absence.

 🗺️

Pattern Diagnosis

Reading the whole picture — pulse, tongue, symptoms — rather than a single symptom.

How TCM fits with modern medicine

🏥

Complementary, not a replacement

TCM is best used alongside modern medicine, not instead of it. Serious conditions need modern diagnostics.

🤝

Often combined

In China, TCM and modern medicine frequently work together — for example, acupuncture alongside surgery or chemotherapy.

💬

Communication matters

Tell your main treating team about any TCM you use; some herbal products can interact with medications.
